BACKGROUNDS: Ruin Delver

You have entered The Ruins in search of relics, old-world records, durable metals, or a route to something the City has forgotten. You learned to treat every door, corpse, machine, and patch of dust as a possible warning.

  • Skill Proficiencies. Investigation and Survival
  • Tool Proficiencies. Cartographer's tools and one vehicle or artisan's tool
  • Equipment. A climber's kit, rechargeable flashlight, 2 power cells, filter mask, 2 air filter cartridges, notebook, 5 days of rations, waterskin, and $100

Delver's Route

You can recognize common Ruin markers, abandoned expedition signs, and the difference between ordinary decay and a likely Warp hazard. When planning an expedition, you can usually find a delver, scavenger, or records clerk who knows one recent rumor about the route. The information is incomplete and may be wrong; it never removes the need for an actual expedition.
