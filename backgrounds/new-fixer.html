BACKGROUNDS: New Fixer

You have only recently entered the Fixer profession. You may have completed an Association examination, worked under an Office, or taken your first contract in the Backstreets, but your reputation has not yet outgrown your mistakes.

  • Skill Proficiencies. Choose two from Acrobatics, Athletics, Investigation, Perception, Persuasion, Stealth, and Survival
  • Tool Proficiencies. Thieves' tools or one artisan's tool, plus one vehicle or gaming set
  • Equipment. A backpack, cellphone, identity badge holder, notebook, one tool you are proficient with, common clothes, and $150

Open Contracts

You know how to locate public requests, read an ordinary contract, and identify the nearest Fixer Office or Association desk in a District you know. You can take legal beginner work through an Office or Association, though the work may be dangerous and the pay is not guaranteed. This feature does not grant a Fixer license; obtaining one still follows the City's licensing rules.
