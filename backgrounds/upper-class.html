BACKGROUNDS: Upper Class

You were lucky enough to be born into fortune, with a wealthy and famous family that holds significant influence in at least one sphere. You understand power and have since a young age, and success usually comes to you easily. You've had to bump shoulders with other members of the upper crust for years. Are you a pampered, spoiled rich kid, content to coast on your family's success? Or are you hardworking sort, trying to prove yourself without relying on their net of influence? How many people would recognize your family name?

  • Skill Proficiencies. Persuasion & Deception
  • Languages. Two of your choice
  • Equipment. A valuable piece of jewelry, an unpaid speeding ticket, a set of fine clothing, a next-generation smartphone, and a wallet containing $250

Friends in High Places

It's easy for you to socialize with friends in high places, and people assume you have the right to be wherever you may find yourself. You can get your foot in the door in many prestigious places, even ones that may have their doors locked to the common people. Nepo greets? No, so you can arrange meetings with the rich and famous without too much difficulty.
