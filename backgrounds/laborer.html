BACKGROUNDS: Laborer

Also called a blue collar worker, your job features a lot of manual labor. Years of such work has made you strong physically and mentally, hard to bend and harder to break. Unpretentious and reliable, you are never far from kindred spirits - as long as there's work to do, there're people to do it. Are you a lone contractor, floating from job to job? Are you part of a steady team? Or do you own your own business?

  • Skill Proficiencies. Perception & Performance
  • Tool Proficiencies. Two of your choice
  • Equipment. A set of work clothes, a full toolbox, a good luck charm, a sturdy phone, and a wallet containing $100

Spirit of Mutual Aid

As an experienced worker, it’s always possible for you to gain work in a city, and you can obtain food and lodging for any length of time in exchange for basic work. Additionally, in the spirit of mutual aid, people will stand alongside you in most circumstances, even dangerous ones.
