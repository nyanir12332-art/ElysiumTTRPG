BACKGROUNDS: Busker

Something in your life has driven you to the streets - not to live, but to perform. You are an entertainer at heart, and thrive under an attentive (and heavy-walled) audience. You can be spotted at high-traffic locations all around the city, performing your heart out in hopes of tips from passersby. Most buskers play instruments, but others are singers, magicians, mimes, gymnasts, living statues - anything goes. What do you do, and how do you do it? How successful are you most days?

  • Skill Proficiencies. Performance & Acrobatics
  • Tool Proficiencies. Two of your choice of artisan's tools, musical instruments, or miscellaneous
  • Equipment. An instrument or other relevant tool for your work (disguise kit, mime costume, magician's kit, etc.), a set of common clothing, a trinket gifted by an audience member, a cellphone, and a wallet containing $170

A Good Show

You're always able to find a place to perform, and can earn enough goodwill from passersby that even if you don't get paid, you won't be driven off. You can earn tips for your performances, or secure lodging for a night in exchange for a good show.
