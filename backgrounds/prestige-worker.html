BACKGROUNDS: Prestige Worker

You’ve sunk thousands of gold into training and education, and now you hold a prestigious, much-vaunted career. A lawyer, or doctor, or something else that requires an expensive qualification and many years of training. You may still have some debts to pay off, or you might be riding high on an already-lengthy career. Are you down-to-earth about your title - it’s just part of the job - or do you take an excessive amount of pride in what made you want to go into the field in the first place?

  • Skill Proficiencies. Medicine & Intimidation
  • Languages. Two of your choice
  • Equipment. A relevant textbook, a notebook and pen, a set of business clothing, a cellphone, and a wallet containing $200

Trading Favors

People with your level of skill are always in demand, and your expertise can be used to reap rewards. You can usually get in contact with the right people at the right time. Everyone wants free legal advice, or to know whether their sister-in-law's cousin should get that mole checked out. If you're willing, trading favors may procure significant results.
