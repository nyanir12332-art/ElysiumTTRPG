BACKGROUNDS: Office Worker

Also called a white collar worker, you could be in any of many fields, but your job involves desk work at an actual office. You know how to work in teams both small and large, though the competence of your coworkers tends to vary. As such, you're used to working with, for, and occasionally around people. Do you work in a tiny, dim cubicle farm? Are you a bottom-rung pencil-pusher, or have you worked your way up to the managerial level? Do you long for a better job, or are you content where you are?

  • Skill Proficiencies. Arcana & Stealth
  • Tool Proficiencies. One of your choice and Vehicles (Land)
  • Equipment. A calculator, a pocket calendar full of reminders, a set of business casual clothes, a cellphone, and a wallet containing $120

Person of the People

You're a person of the people, one of the millions who grease the wheels of the economy. As a result of such kinship in normality, other people will freely offer you a place to rest (or hide) among themselves, so long as you don't bring anything untoward to their doorstep.
