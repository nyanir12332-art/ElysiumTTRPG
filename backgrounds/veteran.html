BACKGROUNDS: Veteran

You survived a war, corporate purge, Syndicate campaign, hazardous expedition, or another period of sustained violence. You know how quickly a confident plan becomes a casualty report, and you carry habits that keep you alive when everyone else panics.

  • Skill Proficiencies. Athletics and Intimidation
  • Tool Proficiencies. One vehicle or artisan's tool, and one gaming set
  • Equipment. A service token or old unit patch, a whetstone, common clothes, a notebook of names, a City Card, and $200

Hardened Survivor

You can identify ordinary signs of an ambush, a recently abandoned position, and a group moving with military discipline. In a settlement where your service is known, you can usually find a veteran willing to exchange practical advice, a meal, or a safe place to sleep. This does not guarantee protection, employment, or loyalty.
