BACKGROUNDS: Criminal

Every city has them, and you’re no exception. You have years of experience breaking the law, and doing it well. You might have tried your hand at a law-abiding life, or you might have been born into the dark side. Are you a white collar criminal, engaging in high-stakes art fraud or money laundering? Perhaps some sort of high-tech cybercriminal, able to break into any system? Or are a more classic on-the-ground criminal, like a petty pickpocket, serial arsonist, or even hired killer? If your crimes ever catch up to you, you may be in for a reckoning. Until then, though, the lifestyle agrees with you.

  • Skill Proficiencies. Deception & Stealth
  • Tool Proficiencies. Thieves' tools and one of your choice of miscellaneous kit or artisan's tools
  • Equipment. A relevant criminal tool (laptop, crowbar, lighter & oil, etc.), a burner phone, a set of unremarkable clothes, and a wallet containing $150

Criminal Contacts

You know people. Whether an in-person network, an encrypted chat room, or a complex encoded letter system, you have trustworthy criminal contacts who can relay information, give you a heads up, or even help you track things or people down.
