BACKGROUNDS: Finger Affiliate

Choose one of the Five Fingers below. An affiliate has contacts and conditional protection through that Finger.

Affiliate Options

Thumb Affiliate

You work under the Thumb's protection or provide services to one of its Famiglie. You have learned that the Thumb values rank, formal courtesy, and obedience above personal comfort. You are useful, but you are not yet a Soldato and cannot give orders to Thumb members.

  • Skill Proficiencies. Intimidation and Persuasion
  • Tool Proficiencies. One gaming set or one tool set appropriate to your work
  • Languages. One language of your choice
  • Equipment. Formal black, red, and gold clothing; a Famiglia badge or token; a notebook listing local titles and forms of address; a cellphone; and $120

Hierarchical Courtesy

You can identify practical signs of rank used by the Thumb and most City organizations. When you need to contact a Thumb-affiliated Famiglia, you can usually locate a minor intermediary, request a private meeting, or obtain a modest safe place to wait, provided you have not violated the Thumb's rules. The intermediary will not risk their life, reveal confidential information, or provide expensive equipment.

Index Affiliate

You carry messages, locate people, or perform small tasks for the Index without having received a Prescript ordering you to join. You have seen enough of the Index to understand that a Prescript is not a normal request, and that neither outsiders nor members are permitted to treat one casually.

  • Skill Proficiencies. Insight and Investigation
  • Tool Proficiencies. Calligrapher's supplies or one set of tools used in your work
  • Languages. One language of your choice
  • Equipment. A black formal outfit; a notebook and pencils; a sealed envelope bearing an Index mark; a cellphone; and $100

Prescript Relay

You know how to deliver a message to an Index intermediary without claiming to interpret a Prescript yourself. Once during a long rest in a settlement with an Index presence, you can deliver a short written request for mundane information, a meeting, or a safe route. The response is never guaranteed and may arrive as an indirect instruction. The Index does not provide combat assistance, valuable property, or an explanation of where its commands originate through this feature.

Middle Affiliate

You are protected by a Middle Sibling, work for a Middle-associated family, or have earned the Finger's tolerance by helping one of its members. You are not a Sibling and do not possess the protection granted to someone whose name is recorded in the Middle's Book of Vengeance.

  • Skill Proficiencies. Athletics and Intimidation
  • Tool Proficiencies. One gaming set or one tool set appropriate to your work
  • Languages. One language of your choice
  • Equipment. A durable bright or light-colored jacket; a notebook recording favors and grudges; a chain or keepsake symbolizing solidarity; a cellphone; and $100

Sibling's Introduction

In Backstreets controlled or influenced by the Middle, you can ask a known Sibling or affiliate to introduce you to another member, locate ordinary shelter, or arrange a conversation about a local dispute. The request is conditional: the Sibling expects respect, honesty, and repayment of favors. This feature cannot erase a Vengeance, alter the Book of Vengeance, or protect you from punishment for a serious sin against the Middle.

Ring Affiliate

You supply materials, display work, run errands for a gallery, or create art that the Ring considers worth evaluating. You are associated with the Ring but are not a Student, Docent, or Maestro. The Ring's protection is conditional on your continued usefulness and the judgments of the artists above you.

  • Skill Proficiencies. Deception and Performance
  • Tool Proficiencies. Painter's supplies or one other artisan's tool
  • Languages. One language of your choice
  • Equipment. An artist's tool set and materials; clean white clothing; a sketchbook; a cellphone; and $120

Gallery Access

You can find a Ring-affiliated gallery or an ordinary artist who knows one. You can submit a harmless piece for evaluation, learn who is accepting commissions, and request a place to work for a short time. Gallery staff may offer food, shelter, or a small payment when your work is useful, but they can also reject it or impose a deadline. This does not let you enter a Main Studio, attend a VIP auction, or bypass a Docent or Maestro.

Pinky Affiliate

You are a courier, informant, cover identity, or useful outsider whose work has benefited a hidden Pinky member. You do not know the identities of most Pinky affiliates, and they may not know yours. You have contacts, but you do not have a seat, a title, or a star-forged blade.

  • Skill Proficiencies. Deception and Stealth
  • Tool Proficiencies. Disguise kit or one tool set appropriate to your cover identity
  • Languages. One language of your choice
  • Equipment. A disguise kit; clothing suitable for two social roles; a notebook with deliberately incomplete contact information; a cellphone; and $150

Unmarked Contact

You can leave a message for a Pinky contact through a prearranged dead drop, coded advertisement, or ordinary social encounter. If the contact chooses to respond, they can provide a rumor, a discreet introduction, or a temporary cover identity. They do not reveal the Pinky's internal structure, expose another member, provide a star-forged blade, or intervene in a dangerous conflict merely because you ask. Your contact may give you a task before offering help.

Using These Backgrounds

An affiliate may have contacts among a Finger without being trusted with its secrets. Favors create obligations and protection has limits. A character can work for more than one faction, but the Five Fingers may treat divided loyalties as a serious problem.
