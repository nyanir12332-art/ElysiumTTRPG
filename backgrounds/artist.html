BACKGROUNDS: Artist

You are a modern artist, and have spent a good portion of your life trying to better your craft. You've dedicated blood, sweat, and tears to your art, whatever it is. Are you a starving artist, laboring away at lesser pursuits in order to support your true passion? Or are already making a name for yourself, recognizable by those in the know? Maybe you take commissions to get by, or maybe yours is a fine art not meant to be consumed by the masses.

  • Skill Proficiencies. Perception & Performance
  • Tool Proficiencies. Two of your choice of artisan's tools or musical instruments
  • Equipment. An instrument or set of relevant artist tools (paints and brushes, sculpting tools, a marked-up first draft, etc.), a set of common clothing, a battered cellphone, and a wallet containing $100

Heart of the People

As an artist, you are uniquely in touch with the beating heart of the people, their wants and whims. You know who the movers and shakers of the city are, and the broad strokes of their goals and interests. You have contacts in the art community all over the city, and learn about news and trends much faster than other people.
