BACKGROUNDS: Traveler

One way or another, you've spent much of your life on the move. Maybe you're a train conductor, holding your post as your freight travels across the country. Maybe you're a trucker, in it for the long haul. Maybe you're a sailor or flight attendant, spending most of your waking hours with your feet off the ground. No matter where you are, you're not there long. What made you want to live such a mobile life? Are you on the run from something, or in pursuit of something?

  • Skill Proficiencies. Athletics & Nature
  • Tool Proficiencies. One vehicle (land, sea, or air) of your choice
  • Languages. One of your choice
  • Equipment. A map of your most recent routes, radio, memento from home, a set of common clothes, a cellphone, and a wallet containing $120

Hitch a Ride

It’s never hard for you to hitch a ride from someone in the same field as you. As long as you don’t act up, you and your party can acquire free passage on your respective background vehicle - car, bus, train, plane, ship - though you may be expected to exchange stories with the traveler doing you a favor.
