BACKGROUNDS: Nest Dweller

You grew up in a Nest and were shaped by its Wing's rules, services, surveillance, and expectations. Choose the District where you began. Your chosen option gives a local contact, habit, or cultural detail; it does not grant citizenship, a visa, or immunity from the Wing's laws.

  • Skill Proficiencies. Insight and Persuasion
  • Tool Proficiencies. Choose one tool associated with your District
  • Equipment. Fine or professional clothing, a cellphone, a City Card, identity documents, and $300

Nest Access

You know how to navigate ordinary Nest services, checkpoints, work schedules, and public etiquette in your home District. In that District, you can usually find a former neighbor, coworker, or service contact who can provide a small favor or point you toward the proper office. They will not knowingly risk their Nest status for you.

District of Origin Options

D District

You grew up around D Corp.'s dream services, hotels, and recreational districts. You know how to spot a staged dream experience and have contacts among attendants, technicians, and people who sell private fantasies.

E District

You were raised among E Corp.'s audit towers and record-adjustment services. You understand how people hide failures behind paperwork and can recognize when a public record has been deliberately altered.

F District

You grew up beneath F Corp.'s elevated glass dome, around puzzle-solving Fairies and bridge settlements. You know how to maintain fragile equipment and navigate layered platforms, ladders, and suspended walkways.

G District

You are from G Corp.'s floating Nest, where gravity technology shapes transport, buildings, and work. You understand lift regulations, aerial routes, and the warning signs of a failing gravitic system.

H District

You grew up around H Corp.'s bioengineering clinics and constantly rearranged housing. You can identify common medical marketing, recognize a basic bolus treatment, and know where displaced residents look for shelter after a restructuring.

I District

You came from I District, where the memory of the Pianist and the first Distortion still shapes daily life. You know local performance spaces, memorial customs, and which streets residents avoid after dark.

J District

You grew up in J Corp.'s gambling Nest, where contracts and locks protect both money and freedom. You can recognize ordinary gambling-house etiquette and identify when a deal contains an obvious conceptual or physical restriction.

K District

You were raised around K Corp.'s ampule clinics and productive farms. You know basic wound triage, the difference between a legitimate clinic and a dangerous imitator, and how to find a medical queue that is not completely compromised.

L District

You grew up in the unprotected remains of old L Corp.'s District. You know how residents survive without a stable Wing, where the Middle and the Index exert pressure, and which abandoned facilities are most likely to attract scavengers.

M District

You came from M Corp.'s medical and psychological-care district. You know the language of mental-health treatment, the risks of counterfeit Moonlight Stones, and how to find a quiet room when a person is overwhelmed.

N District

You were raised among N Corp.'s white buildings, schools, and manufactured experiences. You can recognize common experience packages, understand the etiquette of N Corp. classrooms, and tell when someone is performing a borrowed identity.

O District

You grew up in O Corp.'s windowless containment architecture. You are comfortable checking doors, seals, and environmental controls, and you know how quickly a comfortable room can become a carefully designed trap.

P District

You lived in P Corp.'s durable buildings, where the outside can bend while the inside remains safe. You know structural inspection habits and can find residents who understand the local bloodfiend threat and the Wing's failing protection.

Q District

You were raised in the secretive district of Q Corp., where glyphs and talismans are treated as guarded tools. You can identify common protective markings and know when outsiders are being kept away from a district facility for more than ordinary privacy.

R District

You grew up around R Corp.'s factories, hatcheries, and training grounds. You understand basic squad signals, recognize the signs of haste- or strength-focused augmentation, and know how to stay out of a live training lane.

S District

You came from S Corp.'s agricultural territory, where old-world customs and feudal authority shape daily life. You know local titles, harvest obligations, and which officials can be approached without turning a request into a political insult.

T District

You were raised in T Corp.'s sepia-lit district, where time and color are measured resources. You understand ordinary time-credit etiquette, can estimate a delay by local standards, and know which services refuse visitors who cannot pay in time.

U District

You grew up around U Corp.'s portships and the divided Great Lake. You know basic vessel procedures, lake-weather warnings, and the difference between a tourist route and a passage likely to draw a Whale or worse.

V District

You were raised among V Corp.'s broadcasts, networks, and electronic waste. You can identify common obsolete hardware, know where scavengers trade parts, and understand why a device that looks dead may still be watched.

W District

You grew up around W Corp.'s WARP trains and the strict habits of rapid transit. You know platform procedures, transit checkpoints, and the signs that a route has been closed because of Warp contamination or corporate secrecy.

X District

You came from B Corp.'s directly managed District X, where residents assume they are always being observed. You know how people behave under surveillance, how to keep an ordinary conversation private, and when a quiet street is quiet for a reason.

Y District

You were raised in Y District among snow, garbage, and Warp-contaminated waste. You know how to layer clothing and filters, which shelters remain heated, and how to recognize a contaminated pile before it starts moving.

Z District

You grew up around Z Corp.'s dredging sites and Ruin excavations. You can identify common excavation equipment, recognize a hellhole boundary, and know which work crews leave behind useful warnings instead of simply abandoning a site.
