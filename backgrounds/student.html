BACKGROUNDS: Student

Your life right now is dominated by the fact that you’re in university. Whether a fresh-faced freshman or a burnt-out super senior, you’re enrolled in school in pursuit of a degree and, hopefully, a successful career to follow. Campus life can be full of endless nose-to-the-grindstone study sessions, rampant partying, or anything in between. Are you at a massive state school? A snooty ivy league? Or perhaps a tiny liberal arts college? Is your major your own choice, or were you pressured to go into it?

  • Skill Proficiencies. History & Survival
  • Tool Proficiencies. One of your choice
  • Languages. One of your choice
  • Equipment. A laptop, a student ID card, a letter from your advisor, a set of common clothing, a cellphone, and a wallet containing $90

Pursuit of Knowledge

Your university gives you access to a variety of resources, and it’s not usually hard for you to find an answer to a question. You can pretty easily gain access to databases, libraries, or other scholars in pursuit of knowledge. Additionally, you may be able to leverage the knowledge from your field of study in unique ways.
