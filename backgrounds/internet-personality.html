BACKGROUNDS: Internet Personality

You are one of the myriad types that fall under the hazy sphere of “internet personality.” Primarily, you create content for social media that other people want to watch, be that because it’s well-researched, emotionally enticing, entertaining, or out of sheer bile fascination. Your life, or at least your life as presented to others, is up for scrutiny and consumption at almost all hours of the day. Whether you have a small but dedicated band of followers or a fandom numbering millions, and what type of content you create, is up to you.

  • Skill Proficiencies. Sleight of Hand & Insight
  • Tool Proficiencies. One type of gaming set
  • Languages. One of your choice
  • Equipment. A video camera, a laptop, a branded water bottle, a cellphone, and a wallet containing $170

It's for the Bit

You can get away with a lot of things if you have a video camera in your hands. In most places you can get away with minor bad behavior since people will assume it’s for the bit, and not jump straight to calling the cops. You may also be able to use your recognizability in your favor.
