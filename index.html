A Letter from Ozwald

Sender: Ozwald

May you receive this in your time of need.

The City you live in has made time and life two of its many currencies. Everyday life is a struggle, even for the common person. Corporations will find a way to monetize some aspect of mortal life through magic and law-defying Singularities. Officials and The Head have no interest in helping the needy; they even call this City some sort of cultivation, a garden of their suffering. What lies outside the layers, down in The Ruins, is nothing short of hell itself. Even if you are able to fight your way through the hordes of monstrosities, nothing awaits you beyond The City. There is no Eden, promised land, or heaven. There are only The Wastes, which even the gods turned their backs on.

In this kind of life, people have forsaken their dreams to live today and, in doing so, have no future to look forward to. I am not looking for people who wish for a better future for the City, for their family, or for someone else. What I seek are people who will not—who will never, not even for a moment—compromise or give up their dream. The White Nights and Dark Days have given people the ability to dream even in this place, but there are very few of you.

If you have not given up on your dream, allow me to extend a helping hand. Within this letter are a first-class WARP train ticket and a temporary District D Nest ID. These will grant you safe passage to District D's Nest. When you are there, meet me at what they call the Gates of Horn and Ivory. It is a hotel where I have already made reservations for you.

Of course, you are free to sell the contents of this letter, but I assure you that you will never be this close to finding a thread of hope for the future you wish to witness.
