The setting

A broken realm above the clouds

Elysium is a world of fallen sanctuaries, star-lit ruins, and city-states built on the bones of old divinity. Every campaign begins with a promise. Every promise becomes a test.

Oath-Driven Heroes

Characters are defined by sworn vows, contested ideals, and the people who can still call them home.

Dangerous Expeditions

Journey through living ruins, skyborne frontiers, and sanctums where myth has learned to defend itself.

Consequences That Matter

Victories reshape factions, maps, and relationships, leaving the table with a world that remembers.
