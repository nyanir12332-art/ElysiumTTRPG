Variant: Spell Points

Source: Dungeon Master's Guide

This variant replaces spell slots with spell points. Spell points give a caster more flexibility, at the cost of greater complexity.

Each spell has a point cost based on its level. Cantrips do not require spell points. You spend points equal to the cost of the spell's level when you cast it, and regain all spent points when you finish a long rest.

You can create a spell of any level up to your maximum spell level as long as you have enough points to pay its cost. You can't spend points to cast above that maximum.

Multiclass Spellcasting

For bards, clerics, druids, sorcerers, wizards, and other full spellcasters, use the character's effective spellcaster level on the Spell Points by Level table. For paladins, rangers, and artificers, use half the character's level in that class, rounded up.

For a fighter with the Eldritch Knight archetype, a rogue with the Arcane Trickster archetype, or another subclass that grants spellcasting to a class without it, divide the character's level in that class by three and round up before consulting the table.

Warlocks and features that grant pact magic use the Spell Points and Max Spell Level shown on their class table. Spell points do not combine with a separate spell-slot pool.
