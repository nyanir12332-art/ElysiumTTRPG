SYSTEMS: Character Creation

Character Creation

Create a character by choosing a class, race, background, and perk. Apply the benefits, proficiencies, equipment, and other choices granted by each selection before play begins.

Ability Scores

Use the standard array from D&D 5e (2014): 15, 14, 13, 12, 10, and 8. Assign one score to each ability: Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma.

After assigning the array, you may decrease an ability score by 2, to a minimum of 4, to increase a different ability score by 1, to a maximum of 20. You may repeat this exchange as long as every decrease and increase follows those limits.

Backstory

Write a backstory that ties your character to the setting. Establish where they come from, what their life in the City has been, and the people, obligations, ambitions, or losses that give them a reason to take part in the campaign. Your class, race, background, and perk should inform that history, but the backstory should also leave room for the character to grow during play.

Intellect Proficiencies

After you make your character, if you have a positive Intelligence modifier, you gain proficiency in a weapon (simple, martial, or basic firearm), skill, language, or artisan tool of your choice for each +1 modifier. Make this decision again whenever you gain a new +1 Intelligence modifier from increasing your Intelligence score through an Ability Score Increase or perks.
