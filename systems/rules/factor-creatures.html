SYSTEMS: RULES: Factor Creatures

Factor are cubes of power that hold the essence of the creature they reside within, though they manifest only shortly after death and quickly dissipate. A Factor creature may use Factor Actions, which demand the players' attention because most are unavoidable or require teamwork to break. A creature's Factor can be harvested through Arcana or proficiency with the appropriate tools. The DC depends on the creature's level. A harvested Factor can be used to craft a Relic whose magical properties are themed around that creature.

Factor Actions

Factor Strike

The aggressor marks creatures as targets, announcing the strike. The strike occurs before the round ends, and the aggressor gains immunity to most conditions during the telegraph. Factor Strikes differ in execution, but often deal a large amount of damage without requiring a roll if nothing stands between the aggressor and the target. If an allied creature is between them, that ally may be prompted to make a check to cancel the strike or take reduced damage in the target's place.

Factor Unlock

The creature enters a state of concentration. If undisturbed for 1 turn, it can take 2 turns during each round for the rest of the fight. If it takes damage equal to at least 20% of its maximum hit points during Factor Unlock, it becomes paralyzed until the end of its next turn. A creature that dies while under the effects of Factor Unlock guarantees the harvest of its Factor.

When a player is down, a non-player creature can target them with a Factor Strike. The strike instantly kills the player if they are still unconscious when it hits. A creature without Factor gains Factor, while a creature with Factor immediately receives the effects of Factor Unlock.
