SYSTEMS: RULES: Intelligence

Intelligence

Intelligence measures mental acuity, accuracy of recall, and the ability to reason.

Intelligence Checks

Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, and Religion reflect aptitude in Intelligence checks.

Intellect Proficiencies

After you make your character, if you have a positive Intelligence modifier, you gain proficiency in a weapon (simple, martial, or basic firearm), skill, language, or an artisan tool of your choice for each +1 modifier. You make this decision again whenever you get a new +1 Intelligence modifier from your Intelligence score increasing through Ability Score Increase or perks.

Spellcasting Ability

Wizards use Intelligence as their spellcasting ability.
