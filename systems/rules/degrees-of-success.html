SYSTEMS: RULES: Degrees of Success

Universal Degrees of Success

Compare the result of a d20 check to its DC or target number. A natural 20 or a result 10 or more above the target is a Critical Success. A natural 1 or a result 10 or more below the target is a Critical Fumble.

Critical Success. Good fortune creates a positive outcome beyond normal success or lessens the consequences of a bad outcome.

Success. The character succeeds normally.

Failure. The character fails and suffers the normal consequences.

Critical Fumble. Bad fortune makes a bad outcome worse or adds a complication determined by the GM.

Ability Checks

Apply the four degrees of success to ability checks. A Critical Success counts as an especially favorable result, while a Critical Fumble creates an especially bad result.

Group Checks

During a group check, a Critical Success counts as two successes and a Critical Fumble counts as two failures.

Attacks

Critical Success. You make a damage roll according to the weapon or unarmed strike and deal double damage.

Success. You make a damage roll according to the weapon or unarmed strike and deal damage.

Critical Fumble. The attack misses and may create a complication determined by the GM.

A critical success against a creature at 0 hit points causes two death-saving-throw failures.

Basic Saving Throws

For weapon damage, spell damage, explosives, and similar harmful effects, use these four saving-throw outcomes unless the effect states otherwise:

ResultDamage Taken
Critical Fumble (roll 1 or fail by 10+)Double damage
FailureFull damage
SuccessHalf damage
Critical Success (roll 20 or succeed by 10+)No damage
