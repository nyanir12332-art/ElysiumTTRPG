SYSTEMS: RULES: Sizes

Sizes

Each creature occupies one of six size categories. A creature's size determines the space it controls in combat; it does not necessarily represent its exact physical dimensions. The space listed below is the area the creature controls on a battle map.

SizeControlled Space
Tiny2.5 by 2.5 feet
Small5 by 5 feet
Medium5 by 5 feet
Large10 by 10 feet
Huge15 by 15 feet
Gargantuan20 by 20 feet or larger

Size and Carrying Capacity

Your normal carrying capacity is your Strength score multiplied by 15 pounds, and the amount you can push, drag, or lift is twice that capacity. Apply the following multiplier to both values based on your size.

SizeCapacity Multiplier
Tiny×0.5
Small×1
Medium×1
Large×2
Huge×4
Gargantuan×8

For example, a Large creature with Strength 18 has a carrying capacity of 540 pounds and can push, drag, or lift up to 1,080 pounds. A feature that says you count as a different size for these limits uses that stated size only for this calculation.

Resizing Non-Relic Items

A non-Relic item can be resized only by a character who meets the requirements to craft non-Relic items. Each size adjustment requires raw materials worth half the item's total value.

An item can be adjusted between Medium and Tiny, between Medium and Large, between Large and Huge, or between Huge and Gargantuan. These adjustments work in either direction. Resizing an item doesn't otherwise change its properties.

Resizing Relics

Relics are magical items, so resizing one changes its physical size without removing or replacing its magical properties.

A Relic can be resized only by a character who meets the requirements to craft Relics. Each size adjustment requires raw materials worth half the Relic's total value.

A Relic can be adjusted between Medium and Tiny, between Medium and Large, between Large and Huge, or between Huge and Gargantuan. These adjustments work in either direction; for example, a Huge Relic can be reduced to Large, or a Large Relic can be enlarged to Huge. Resizing a Relic doesn't otherwise change its properties.

Weapons and Size

Weapons are sized for their intended wielder. A creature can't use a weapon that is two or more size categories larger or smaller than itself. A creature has disadvantage on attack rolls made with a weapon that is one size category larger or smaller than itself, except that Small creatures can use Medium weapons and Medium creatures can use Small weapons without disadvantage.

When a weapon is larger than Medium, add one additional set of its normal damage dice for each size category above Medium. For example, a Large dagger deals 2d4 damage, a Huge dagger deals 3d4 damage, and a Gargantuan dagger deals 4d4 damage. Add ability modifiers and other bonuses only once unless another rule says otherwise.
