SYSTEMS: RULES: Strength

Strength

Strength measures bodily power, athletic training, and the extent to which you can exert raw physical force.

Strength Checks

Strength checks model attempts to lift, push, pull, break, or force your body through a space. Athletics reflects aptitude in these checks.

Attack Rolls and Damage

You add your Strength modifier to melee attack and damage rolls.

Lifting and Carrying

Your carrying capacity is your Strength score multiplied by 15. You can push, drag, or lift up to twice that capacity.

Variant: Encumbrance

The rules for lifting and carrying are intentionally simple. This variant provides more detailed rules for determining how a character is hindered by the weight of equipment. When using this variant, ignore the Strength column of the Armor table.

If you carry weight in excess of 5 times your Strength score, you are encumbered, which reduces your speed by 10 feet.

If you carry weight in excess of 10 times your Strength score, up to your maximum carrying capacity, you are instead heavily encumbered, which reduces your speed by 20 feet and gives you disadvantage on ability checks, attack rolls, and saving throws that use Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution.
