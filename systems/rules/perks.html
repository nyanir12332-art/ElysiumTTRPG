SYSTEMS: RULES: Perks

A perk represents a talent or an area of expertise that gives a character special capabilities. It embodies training, experience, and abilities beyond what a class provides.

At each level, you gain the choice to select a perk of which you meet the prerequisites for, if any. If in any case your level is decreased, choose a perk you have gained and remove it from your character sheet.
