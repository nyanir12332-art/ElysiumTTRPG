SYSTEMS: RULES: Inspiration

At the start of the session, each player gains an inspiration. A players may gain additional inspiration by advancing the plot of the current session.

Using Inspiration

If you have inspiration, you can expend it after you make an attack roll, saving throw, or ability check. Spending your inspiration allows you to reroll that die and you may keep the new result unless stated otherwise.

Drama Cards

When a player gains inspiration while already having an inspiration, when a session ends with a player not having used their inspiration, or when a player has contributed into the recording of a session's events, the GM awards that player with a drama card. Drama cards are cards that can change fate, they can be played at any time during a session to steer the plot in a direction that benefits the player. Once a drama card is used, it is returned to the GM. Drama cards persists until you use them, but at any point, if you have more than 3 drama cards, you must choose and discard one of them.
