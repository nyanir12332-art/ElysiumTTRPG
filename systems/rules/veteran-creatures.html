SYSTEMS: RULES: Veteran Creatures

A veteran creature can do things that ordinary creatures can't. It has a pool of points called Technique Points it can expend to take special actions outside its turn.

If a creature assumes the form of a veteran creature, such as through a spell, it doesn't gain that form's veteran techniques.

Veteran Creature Minions

Veteran creatures may be accompanied by minions. These minions are numerous and serve as a resource of the veteran creature. A minion has 1 hit point.

Veteran Techniques

A veteran creature can take a certain number of special actions—called veteran techniques—outside its turn. Only one veteran technique option can be used at a time. It can forgo using them, and it can't use them while incapacitated or otherwise unable to take actions.
