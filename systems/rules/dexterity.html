SYSTEMS: RULES: Dexterity

Dexterity

Dexterity measures agility, reflexes, and balance.

Dexterity Checks

Acrobatics, Sleight of Hand, and Stealth reflect aptitude in Dexterity checks.

Attack Rolls and Damage

You add Dexterity to ranged attacks and may use it for finesse weapons.

Armor Class

Depending on your armor, you might add some or all of your Dexterity modifier to Armor Class.

Initiative

At the beginning of combat, you roll initiative by making a Dexterity check.

Hiding

When you try to hide, make a Dexterity (Stealth) check contested by Wisdom (Perception).
