SYSTEMS: RULES: Charisma

Charisma

Charisma measures your ability to interact effectively with others, including confidence and eloquence.

Charisma Checks

Deception, Intimidation, Performance, and Persuasion reflect aptitude in Charisma checks.

Spellcasting Ability

Bards, paladins, sorcerers, and warlocks use Charisma as their spellcasting ability.
