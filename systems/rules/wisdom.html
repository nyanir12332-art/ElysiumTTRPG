SYSTEMS: RULES: Wisdom

Wisdom

Wisdom reflects attunement to the world and represents perceptiveness and intuition.

Wisdom Checks

Animal Handling, Insight, Medicine, Perception, and Survival reflect aptitude in Wisdom checks.

Spellcasting Ability

Clerics, druids, and rangers use Wisdom as their spellcasting ability.
