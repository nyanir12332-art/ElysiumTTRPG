SYSTEMS: ADVENTURING: Resting

Heroic though they might be, adventurers can't spend every hour of the day in the thick of exploration, social interaction, and combat. They need rest--time to sleep and eat, tend their wounds, refresh their minds and spirits for spellcasting, and brace themselves for further adventure.

Adventurers can take one short term rest per day. A long term rest is completed after three short term rests without fighting between them.

Short Term Rest

A short term rest is a period of downtime exactly 8 hours long, during which a character does nothing more strenuous than eating, drinking, reading, sleeping, and tending to wounds. A character can benefit from only one short term rest per day.

A character can spend one or more Hit Dice at the end of a short term rest, up to the character's maximum number of Hit Dice, which is equal to the character's level. For each Hit Die spent in this way, the player rolls the die and adds the character's Constitution modifier to it. The character regains hit points equal to the total. The player can decide to spend an additional Hit Die after each roll.

Finishing a short term rest reduces the character's exhaustion by 1 level, provided the character is given and consumes food during the rest. Without food, the short term rest does not reduce exhaustion.

If a character completes a short term rest in a fortified location that is both comfortable and safe, with a guarantee that no enemies are present, that short term rest counts as a completed long term rest and grants all of its effects and benefits. Examples include the character's own Office or a highly priced hotel in a Nest.

Long Term Rest

A long term rest is completed by finishing three short term rests without fighting between them. Fighting before the third short term rest is completed breaks the sequence, and the count begins again with the next completed short term rest.

At the end of a long term rest, a character regains all lost hit points, regains all spent Hit Dice, and clears all exhaustion. Other features and resources that recharge after a long term rest are also restored.

A character must have at least 1 hit point at the start of the final short term rest in the sequence to gain the long term rest's benefits.
