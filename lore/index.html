Elysium Timeline

The Old World

Documents that precede The Empire's records talk about a world without magic, a world of seven continents that's connected by an ocean. For clarity, we'll refer to this world as The Old World going forward. The Old World was said to be detached from other realms, including the elemental planes or the influence of gods and outer gods. They worshipped idols and gods through practice of scriptures and saw good fortune as signs of their god's attention. They were also an advanced race comparable to the era of the later patent wars. Rumors amongst elvenkind talk about The Old World being flooded after first contact of a deity, though dwarves and some secluded goblin settlements tell tales of The Old World persisting as the forgotten foundation of our own world. The thought of The Old World being underneath our planet brings many radical ideas, but connects the dots of some documents we recovered from The Empire about The Empyrean War.

The Empyrean War

The Empyrean War has been a matter of debate for a few centuries after the remains of The Empire was found and records of the war have been openly confiscated and restricted both by The Followers of The Pantheon and The Head. What is known about the war is that it was a war of the gods, their apostles, and their followers, of which there was no victor. Many agree it to be cause of why the world outside of Elysium is currently inhospitable for most creatures, but only a few dare to speculate about the cause of the war as it is a sensitive topic that could be seen as an offense to The Followers of The Pantheon.

The Empire

Records of The Empire state as a fact that it is a civilisation where it was directly influenced by a deity and where they gifted the bloodline of the throne the blood of the gods. The Empire is seen as the victor of The Empyrean War, not because it was confirmed with evidence, but because of the lack of records of any other settlement or kingdom that existed in its era. Almost all long lived races have no memory of The Empire or the world before the construction of Elysium, as if an act of divine intervention prevented its recollection, so we cannot confirm if the populace of Elysium are direct descendants of the people of The Empire.

The Mana Fallout

After retrieving records and books from The Empire, later books, or well, the last books that the imperials wrote was about a fallout of wild magic, where forests errode or decay in seconds, rivers of water become portals to other planes, and a rolling dense suffocating fog of mana blanketed the entire planet. Many speculate that it was the early stages of the apocalypse The Empire named The Mana Fallout. Treatment records describe races aligned with magic or directly inheriting bloodlines of magical beings such as true dragons or elementals started to age quicker- no, decay. Those that spent their life around magic would see similar effects, elves, genasi, druids, wizards, and many more began to age as if they were goblins. Many of the elderly died of quick, some races were pushed close to extinction and were forced to built bunkers. Bunkers we first thought as vaults or neighbouring ruins of Elysium.

Formation of The Lunar Wastes

Had all went well with the plans of The Imperials to bunker down, the world would have recovered after a few centuries and persevered, if not for the ocean becoming a planar doorway for celestial bodies to pass through and suddenly merge into the planet from the inside out. The deities prophesized of the event and attempted to preserve what little of the realm they could as well as their follower. The sudden inner collision of the ocean of the world birthing one of the planet's moons would be the end of our world if not for the direct intervention of the gods. The aftermath of the collision left the remains of the realm in a flux, creating the surface of the planet we now call The Lunar Wastes.

1 AE Discovery of The Ruins

At some point, our ancestors had begun to leave their bunkers to search for a new home, many died in the pilgrimage but the gods directed their faithful to a land of promise. The Ruins of Elysium which stood above the wastes and the waves of errosion. They were directed to build a city, the last bastion of mortals to continue living within the realm.

14 AE The City of Elysium

When The City of Elysium was raised, the gods of minor portfolios began to direct their followers to other realms to seek refuge, though a collection of greater gods make a Pantheon as an agreement to aid the development of Elysium collectively. Technology and magic were quickly improved, but the power of their followers amongst the survivors of The Lunar Wastes began to rise sharply...

114 AE Patent War

The Patent War was a conflict of property, the scriptures, technology, and traditions of an entire community or religion copied by idol worshipers and those that worship outer influences that was not of the divine. They stole chants, hymns, magic, and identities and aligned themselves against the followers of the gods. It started a never ending arms race between The Pantheon's followers and the rest of the populace who wanted to covet the power of faith of The Pantheon but live outside of its order.

667 AE Construct Uprising

When technology was reached, the unfaithful worshippers of non-divinity began to construct bodies of free labor to fight against the evergrowing faith of the people of The Pantheon. The first non-mortal was made in Elysium, it wasn't the first in history as there had been many records of automated humanoid machinery in the history of The Empire, but these machines were built to advise, act, and learn from The Pantheon's people. They began to mirror emotion, powers, and faith directed to gods outside of The Pantheon, this led to the liberation of artificial intelligence from the confines of their programming, aided by outer gods to take over Elysium. This conflict did the impossible, uniting the unfaithful and faithful factions of Elysium.

704 AE The Taboos

After the Construct Uprising started, The Pantheon's followers and a new government body called The Head, worked together to surpress constructs of all kinds. Destroying aritifiial intelligence and purging it from all devices, dumping every line of code into the waves of The Lunar Wastes to erode into non-existence. Afterwards, regulations against the creation of constructs later formed, a specific set of regulations called "The Taboos" were set into motion. Taboos were actions that were forbidden from being commited within the city, the act of doing any of the taboos would lead to immediate execution, namely the creation of mechanical non-decomposing bodies that had autonomy and the use of technology or magic patented by companies, religious groups, workshops, etc. that reside in the city.

744 AE The Smoke War

The Smoke War was a war between mortals and those that didn't want to be. The war started from a new corporation being a massive energy providor for large sections of the city. The polution of its method to generate energy was fair, but it only grew worse and soon the section that the corporation resided in became inhospitable. The officials of the section, instead of putting backlash on the corporation, began to produce products to help cope with living within the section to profit off of their the common folk's suffering. Everything went well until the pollution got to the point of overflowing onto other sections of the city till breatheable air became a commodity. War broke out and what spilled onto the battlefield weren't humanoids, but humanoids shaped into insectoids as if they were clay put into a mold of insects. These were the products they were being sold by the section to help cope with the smoke, not inhalers or masks, but body part replacements. Despite their grotesque appearance, Elysium recognized these replacements as products of the section and official prosthesis.
