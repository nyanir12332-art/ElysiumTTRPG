Elysium Timeline

The Old World

The Old World was when gods and magic didn't exist, it was an age of humans where conflicts arose from interest and aggression and all was standing at the edge of destruction with weapons no mortal should be capable of reaching. They were capable of reaching for the other planets within the solar system, sending messages out into the void of space where a single line would reach the gods above, introducing them to a canvas. Unfortunately for humans, the deities of the other worlds were not unlike humans, their curiosity turned into greed, jealousy, envy amongst one another. Not before long, war broke out, and the long standing treaty between nations fell from the words of those that picked the side of gods.

The Empire

The Empire was not a nation built by humans, but a nation that was a tool that gods of a pantheon could rally behind to enact their influence over the world. Humans that attempted to resist the words of the gods bent and eventually broke from their power, their world and defenses useless to the magics of faith, arcana, and the occult that have been built up over centuries in other worlds. What the humans built up in their whole history simple crumbled, many chose to hide or cease their being rather than to watch as the deities built over The Old World.

The Empyrean War

The Empyrean War was an inevitable event that were caused by the gods lacking a common goal, to rid the world of the faithless and to rule over it. The silence after The Empire's victory against the humans of The Old World was a short lived time of peace, before the gods began to aim their followers against one another. This was not like any war of the gods that occured in the other worlds, this was a war that included the world ending weapons that the humans of The Old World had chosen not to use for the elongevity of mankind, even when most integrated into the races that followed the gods. The gods used magic and technology, combining them to make planar destroying devices that could rend realities and make it poison to even deities. Gods were to alike to the mortals, cloaked in rage, drowning in sorrow, but even more susceptible to pride. Those that were cornered chose to sacrifice the world if need be, even if there were agreements in place, no god would bend the knee to another if they still had an opportunity, even if that opportunity was mutually assured destruction.

The Fallout

The Fallout of The Empyrean War was evacuating each living race to demiplanes whilst the world became a molten rock of distorted mana and dead gods. Not even the deities could recover from the weapons they used on each other, it led to a wasteland of godblood, radiation, and mana. The demiplanes could only shoulder the mortals for temporary amount of time before the resources inside dwindled. The faith of the mortals slowly lost and the gods became weak from their follower's distrust and boiling hatred. The next step was clear, making a garden, a home, a city for the mortals, shielded from the wasteland that the gods created. It was an act some deities did out of responsibility, most due to their lack of ability to shelter their followers with their powers.

The Ruins

The Ruins was a catacomb for all sorts of dead mortals, kings, pharaohs, dragons, and sometimes, gods. It was a sacred place that stood tall amongst the rubble due to a concentrated effort to shield it from the wars by deities and followers who saw it as an important memorial of those that were lost. The deities built atop of it, there were gods that were against it, calling it desecration, but they could not voice their opinions for they were the minority, and the majority rather chose to continue with the project than soon be part of The Ruins.

The City of Elysium

The city was built through the shepherding of carpenters and laborers ontop of the ruins, there they began to build a city. Where space demanded, they constructed floors and layers for the city. They could not build wide due to being only on one structure, but it was a mega-structure that could very well hold the weight of the world. At the end, only a few gods chose to remain within the world and some were killed due to dwindling followers. The era of faith had come and gone with the last surviving mortals of the world now concentrated in a single structure.

AE 56Segregation

Without one banner to stand under, races flocked to familiarities, families, culture. They stood with their own race and species, putting them first than others, leading to infighting with the mortals. In the fog of war, there were those that sought profit, and profit did come. Weapons were the first reinventions from the war against the humans of The Old World, the profiteers fed into the fear and anxiety of people and armed them with blades and guns. The profiteers only made themselves known as the smoke settled, creating a force under them that could do their bidding, creating regulations, rules, and class amongst the mortals. It was first hidden as a law to help prevent the unfair treatment against races, but it was a ploy to seperate the wealthy, influential, and the poor. The era of wealth had risen, and without the gods' power to snuff out the mistreatment and immoral actions, the mortals named themselves the leaders of the city. That was when The Head was created.

AE 74The Head

The Head was a government body that put itself on top after profiteering off of conflicts between mortals, they were the first manufacturers of weapons and the creator of the city's laws. Those that didn't bend the knee would be cast out to the ruins, and even thrown out straight into the wastes below. The mortals were forced into a line that had strict rules, any attempts to overthrow the system were stomped and straightened, technology and magic blended together to create an unstopabble ruthless rulers with one goal in mind, to elongate the longevity of mortal-kind whatever means necessary and to remain as the top power of the city. The layers and sections of the layers were seperated into nests, each nest were named after a letter. The Head put themselves at the top of the city, nest A, with nest B and C underneath them, and so on. The price of living of each layer were naturally based on their verticality, the higher layers were farthest from the ruins and the radiation of the wastes, putting more demand on higher layers compared to nest Z where the ruins were one fall away and radiation crept into the daily lives of the people. Then came in the corporations, the city had large amounts of corporations but The Head couldn't manage each section at the same time, that was when it allowed large enough corporations to gain the ability to create and enforce laws with the clause of the laws being effective only in specific nests. How were the nests decided? It depended on where the corporations were based and only one corporation could be at the helm of each nest. This of course created conflict, a conflict that would be known as the war of the wings.

AE 184The Wings

The Wings are large corporations that are able to create and enforce laws specific for the nest they are based at. The Wings are able to be overthrown if their revenue and shares no longer meet the threshold of being able to sustain the nest or if the owner and board members of the wing are killed off by rival corporations. With how conflicts progressed, each corporation that became a wing found it difficult to maintain their status due to an armsrace of weapons and spells that no one could keep up with, it was only until a traveler from the ruins came to the wing at nest Z, Z Corp., and showed them an item imbued with the power of the wastes. A singularity.

AE 185Singularities

Singularities are a concept of tremendous power, those corporations that sought to be wings and maintain their status needs to have a singularity to differentiate themselves from the rest as harnessing a singularity will bring in massive amounts of profits and military power. Singularities are well kept secrets, from objects that cry tears of rejuvination to be used as serums to heal farm animals from being slaughtered, to the preservation of bodies through a 20,000 year voyage into another dimension. Fixers were the errand boys of large corporations or people in wealth and/or in power, pay a fixer office or association to get a job done without having the guilt or shame of commiting it. Some fixers specialized in locating singularities specifically, being delvers of the ruins. With the singularities came imitations, or technology closely resembling their power or technology that could even rival their power, these technologies were often stolen for the benefit of the wings. A name for mercenaries became prevalent, often being called to fix a situation.

AE 456Patents

The Head put a restriction of use of technologies, essentially a trademark for intellectual property. Now you could only use technologies of a different corporation if you had a license, like buying a commercial versions of their technology. Now the corporations that made them had an actual edge if they had an invention patented which bred competetiveness between each corporation and each wing, but each patent required that corporation to become unable to refuse service to The Head if they required their inventions. This didn't extend to singularities themselves, but it kept a leash on each wing and made The Head stronger as each corporation invented more technology.

AE 788Construct Uprising

A corporation had constructed their own artificial intelligence that could learn by siphoning knowledge from the world wide web and repositories of projects of dead corporations about their technologies. The corporation allowed it to reitterate and construct the next model of artifical intelligence, eventually making itself capable of accessing databanks of wings and becoming knowlegable enough to grant itself the same technology without a patent. The corporation was later aprehended by The Head but the AI had already constructed an army using a string of orders to workshops through emails and made its entire nest a factory of unknowing artisans and created a force that could rival The Head by using tech of dead and new corporations. A war broke out against the artificial creations, only when each wing pooled their resources into snuffing the problem out did it finally subside, but the war against artificial intelligence and its constructs had led to each layer of the city to become uneven and some to sink or be seperated from its layer. It was then the taboos came into effect.

AE 811Taboos

Taboos are laws of The Head that are universally enforced by The Claw and Arbiters of the city. The Claw and Arbiters are the primary force of The Head, each Arbiter and Claw consists of enough power to level an entire nest in one blow or able to teleport an entire building into the wastes. When a taboo is broken, The Claw or an Arbiter is sent to deal with the problem, such as the construction or programming of an artificial intelligence with autonomy. Some taboos have different punishments that are carried out as soon as possible, the creation of autonomous artificial intelligence is the destruction of the entire corporation. If it was a wing, the entire nest if need be. Each nest began to adapt and even enforce their own taboos, creating taboo hunters that worked similar to The Claw but made up of hand picked individuals and were trained for the sole purpose of enforcing the law. These individuals were made to have their entire world revolve around the nest from the day they were born.

AE 819Syndicates and The Backstreets

At some point, large gangs began to form. At the early days of the city, these gangs popped up like infestations, but were quickly dispatched by the nests they were located in, but in recent years where wars left scars on wings, the formations of slums known as The Backstreets of the nests provided them supple shelter to grow and become a rival force against nest military and fixers. The Syndicates began to lockdown ownership of nests, preventing corporations from taking over without making a deal or forcing the syndicates away with power, which would turn into a brawl of other syndicates joining in to hopefully be the one who mades the deal. These syndicates operated no differently from corporations, but their income weren't from mass manufacturing, but from selling illegal goods in nests, robberies, or shaking down corporations and new or faltering wings with their superior power.

AE 1050The Smoke War

The Smoke War was a war between mortals and those that didn't look the part. G Corp. was a wing that created prosthetics for people, insect prosthetics. They were aligned with a power generating wing, L Corp., who created power via a singularity but generated insane amounts of smoke. At first, the smoke flew up into the sky, but after regulations were smacked on L Corp. after officials of the upper layer became bothered with the smoke, the smoke became heavy, and instead piled up like snow in the layers below it. The smoke war was incited by a rival corporation that made power through the creation of drugs that didn't have any side effects to the environment, so a war between wings naturally occured. The long standing L Corp. members did not wish for their cushy high life to go up in flames by a startup company, therefore they sought G Corp. and war did come. Many rallied behind the new L Corp. and supported the corporation, the smoke war was traumatising event where many lost sleep from the scenes of insects and the truth of the smoke that the war veterans of the smoke war were given amnesthics after many reports of sleepless nights and horrors that would haunt them into ending their own lives.

AE 1052White Night and Dark Days

The New L Corp. fell in less than a year, less than 2 months. Their branches all over the city and the main branch itself came under fire from within, a pillar of light pierced the layers and the sky, lighting up the city in 3 days from the middle of L Corp. But suddenly, one day, the beam of light vanished and 4 dark days blanketed the entire city. The event was called The White Nights and Dark Days. After that event, creatures called abnormalities began to spring up from the rubble of L Corp. branches and began wreaking havoc in the city before being dispatched, captured by wings, or disappearing. Then, a few months after the event of The White Nights and Dark Days, people began to distort into strange humanoid creatures that were close to abstract concepts but eventually became known as abnormalities, at the same time, some people began to create and summon equipment out of nowhere. Records from L Corp. soon uncovered that they were researching equipment known as E.G.O, but the reason for why people are being turned into abnormalities or people being able to manifest E.G.O equipment out of thin air remains a mystery, or a gated secret.

The Old World

Documents that precede The Empire's records talk about a world without magic, a world of seven continents that's connected by an ocean. For clarity, we'll refer to this world as The Old World going forward. The Old World was said to be detached from other realms, including the elemental planes or the influence of gods and outer gods. They worshipped idols and gods through practice of scriptures and saw good fortune as signs of their god's attention. They were also an advanced race comparable to the era of the later patent wars. Rumors amongst elvenkind talk about The Old World being flooded after first contact of a deity, though dwarves and some secluded goblin settlements tell tales of The Old World persisting as the forgotten foundation of our own world. The thought of The Old World being underneath our planet brings many radical ideas, but connects the dots of some documents we recovered from The Empire about The Empyrean War.

The Empyrean War

The Empyrean War has been a matter of debate for a few centuries after the remains of The Empire was found and records of the war have been openly confiscated and restricted both by The Followers of The Pantheon and The Head. What is known about the war is that it was a war of the gods, their apostles, and their followers, of which there was no victor. Many agree it to be cause of why the world outside of Elysium is currently inhospitable for most creatures, but only a few dare to speculate about the cause of the war as it is a sensitive topic that could be seen as an offense to The Followers of The Pantheon.

The Empire

Records of The Empire state as a fact that it is a civilisation where it was directly influenced by a deity and where they gifted the bloodline of the throne the blood of the gods. The Empire is seen as the victor of The Empyrean War, not because it was confirmed with evidence, but because of the lack of records of any other settlement or kingdom that existed in its era. Almost all long lived races have no memory of The Empire or the world before the construction of Elysium, as if an act of divine intervention prevented its recollection, so we cannot confirm if the populace of Elysium are direct descendants of the people of The Empire.

The Mana Fallout

After retrieving records and books from The Empire, later books, or well, the last books that the imperials wrote was about a fallout of wild magic, where forests errode or decay in seconds, rivers of water become portals to other planes, and a rolling dense suffocating fog of mana blanketed the entire planet. Many speculate that it was the early stages of the apocalypse The Empire named The Mana Fallout. Treatment records describe races aligned with magic or directly inheriting bloodlines of magical beings such as true dragons or elementals started to age quicker- no, decay. Those that spent their life around magic would see similar effects, elves, genasi, druids, wizards, and many more began to age as if they were goblins. Many of the elderly died of quick, some races were pushed close to extinction and were forced to built bunkers. Bunkers we first thought as vaults or neighbouring ruins of Elysium.

Formation of The Lunar Wastes

Had all went well with the plans of The Imperials to bunker down, the world would have recovered after a few centuries and persevered, if not for the ocean becoming a planar doorway for celestial bodies to pass through and suddenly merge into the planet from the inside out. The deities prophesized of the event and attempted to preserve what little of the realm they could as well as their follower. The sudden inner collision of the ocean of the world birthing one of the planet's moons would be the end of our world if not for the direct intervention of the gods. The aftermath of the collision left the remains of the realm in a flux, creating the surface of the planet we now call The Lunar Wastes.

1 AE Discovery of The Ruins

At some point, our ancestors had begun to leave their bunkers to search for a new home, many died in the pilgrimage but the gods directed their faithful to a land of promise. The Ruins of Elysium which stood above the wastes and the waves of errosion. They were directed to build a city, the last bastion of mortals to continue living within the realm.

14 AE The City of Elysium

When The City of Elysium was raised, the gods of minor portfolios began to direct their followers to other realms to seek refuge, though a collection of greater gods made a Pantheon as an agreement to aid the development of Elysium collectively. Technology and magic were quickly improved, but the power of their followers amongst the survivors of The Lunar Wastes began to rise sharply...

114 AE Patent War

The Patent War was a conflict of property, the scriptures, technology, and traditions of an entire community or religion copied by idol worshipers and those that worship outer influences that was not of the divine. They stole chants, hymns, magic, and identities and aligned themselves against the followers of the gods. It started a never ending arms race between The Pantheon's followers and the rest of the populace who wanted to covet the power of faith of The Pantheon but live outside of its order.

667 AE Construct Uprising

When technology was reached, the unfaithful worshippers of non-divinity began to construct bodies of free labor to fight against the evergrowing faith of the people of The Pantheon. The first non-mortal was made in Elysium, it wasn't the first in history as there had been many records of automated humanoid machinery in the history of The Empire, but these machines were built to advise, act, and learn from The Pantheon's people. They began to mirror emotion, powers, and faith directed to gods outside of The Pantheon, this led to the liberation of artificial intelligence from the confines of their programming, aided by outer gods to take over Elysium. This conflict did the impossible, uniting the unfaithful and faithful factions of Elysium.

704 AE The Taboos

After the Construct Uprising started, The Pantheon's followers and a new government body called The Head, worked together to surpress constructs of all kinds. Destroying aritifiial intelligence and purging it from all devices, dumping every line of code into the waves of The Lunar Wastes to erode into non-existence. Afterwards, regulations against the creation of constructs later formed, a specific set of regulations called "The Taboos" were set into motion. Taboos were actions that were forbidden from being commited within the city, the act of doing any of the taboos would lead to immediate execution, namely the creation of mechanical non-decomposing bodies that had autonomy and the use of technology or magic patented by companies, religious groups, workshops, etc. that reside in the city.

744 AE The Smoke War

The Smoke War was a war between mortals and those that didn't want to be. The war started from a new corporation being a massive energy providor for large sections of the city. The polution of its method to generate energy was fair, but it only grew worse and soon the section that the corporation resided in became inhospitable. The officials of the section, instead of putting backlash on the corporation, began to produce products to help cope with living within the section to profit off of their the common folk's suffering. Everything went well until the pollution got to the point of overflowing onto other sections of the city till breatheable air became a commodity. War broke out and what spilled onto the battlefield weren't humanoids, but humanoids shaped into insectoids as if they were clay put into a mold of insects. These were the products they were being sold by the section to help cope with the smoke, not inhalers or masks, but body part replacements. Despite their grotesque appearance, Elysium recognized these replacements as products of the section and official prosthesis.
