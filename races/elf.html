RACES: Elf

Elves hold a long-established place in City schools, arts, archives, and specialized professions. Fey ties are treated as respectable where other planar origins are not. Some tallfolk circles retain prejudices against short peoples and against Shadowfell, abyssal, and infernal residents, while making exceptions for fey-linked elves.

  • Ability Score Increase. Choose one ability score to increase by 2 and a different ability score to increase by 1, or choose three different ability scores to increase by 1.
  • Age. Although elves reach physical maturity at about the same age as humans, the elven understanding of adulthood goes beyond physical growth to encompass worldly experience. An elf typically claims adulthood and an adult name around the age of 100 and can live to be 750 years old.
  • Personality. Most elves are reflective, curious, and slow to make permanent judgments. A smaller number are intensely practical and impatient with tradition, particularly those shaped by short-lived City crises.
  • Size. Elves range from under 5 to over 6 feet tall and have slender builds. Your size is Medium.
  • Speed. Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
  • Darkvision. Accustomed to twilit forests and the night sky, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
  • Fey Ancestry. You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put you to sleep.
  • Trance. Elves do not sleep. Instead they meditate deeply, remaining semi-conscious, for 4 hours a day. The Common word for this meditation is "trance." While meditating, you dream after a fashion; such dreams are actually mental exercises that have become reflexive after years of practice. After resting in this way, you gain the same benefit a human would from 8 hours of sleep.
  • Keen Senses. You have proficiency in the Perception skill.
  • Languages. You can speak, read, and write Common and Elvish.

Dark Elf

Dark elves carry a history of exclusion that makes trust difficult and community essential. In the City, they are judged more fairly by people who share work and risk than by old surface stories.

  • Superior Darkvision. Your darkvision has a range of 120 feet, instead of 60.
  • Sunlight Sensitivity. You have disadvantage on attack rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight when you, the target of the attack, or whatever you are trying to perceive is in direct sunlight.
  • Drow Magic. You know the Dancing Lights cantrip. When you reach 3rd level, you can cast the Faerie Fire spell once with this trait and regain the ability to do so when you finish a long rest. When you reach 5th level, you can cast the Darkness spell once and regain the ability to do so when you finish a long rest. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells.
  • Drow Weapon Training. You have proficiency with rapiers, shortswords, and Hand Crossbows.

High Elf

High elves are associated with formal education, magical study, and archival work. Their prestige can create distance, but many use their access to protect rather than exclude.

  • Cantrip. You know one cantrip of your choice from the Wizard spell list. Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for it.
  • Elf Weapon Training. You have proficiency with the longsword, shortsword, shortbow, and longbow.
  • Extra Language. You can read, speak, and write one additional language of your choice.

Wood Elf

Wood elves preserve ecological knowledge inside a built environment. They are often first to notice when a district water, air, or green spaces are being neglected.

  • Elf Weapon Training. You have proficiency with the longsword, shortsword, shortbow, and longbow.
  • Fleet of Foot. Your base walking speed increases to 35 feet.
  • Mask of the Wild. You can attempt to hide even when you are only lightly obscured by foliage, heavy rain, falling snow, mist, and other natural phenomena.
