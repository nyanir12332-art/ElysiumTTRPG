RACES: Human

Humans remain the City largest and most institutionally entrenched people, and many laws and public spaces were built around human assumptions. That history has left deep-seated human chauvinism: some humans treat themselves as the original race and other peoples as later intrusions. It remains a common source of exclusion, even as countless human families reject it.

  • Ability Score Increase. Choose one ability score to increase by 2 and a different ability score to increase by 1, or choose three different ability scores to increase by 1.
  • Age. Humans reach adulthood in their late teens and live less than a century.
  • Personality. Most humans are shaped more by district, class, and family than by ancestry. A smaller but influential minority embraces human chauvinism and treats other peoples as outsiders; many other humans actively oppose that belief.
  • Size. Humans vary widely in height and build, from barely 5 feet to well over 6 feet tall. Regardless of your position in that range, your size is Medium.
  • Speed. Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
  • Specialized. You gain 1 perk and proficiency in a skill of your choice.
  • Languages. You can speak, read, and write Common and one extra language of your choice. Humans typically learn the languages of other peoples they deal with, including obscure dialects. They are fond of sprinkling their speech with words borrowed from other tongues: Orc curses, Elvish musical expressions, Dwarvish military phrases, and so on.
