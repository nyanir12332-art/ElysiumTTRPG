RACES: Tiefling

Tieflings are City-born people whose infernal heritage is often mistaken for a moral verdict. Families and officials may treat them as if Hell is a crime they committed, so tiefling communities build safety through kinship and hard-won local trust.

  • Ability Score Increase. Choose one ability score to increase by 2 and a different ability score to increase by 1, or choose three different ability scores to increase by 1.
  • Age. Tieflings mature at the same rate as humans but live a few years longer.
  • Personality. Most tieflings are self-possessed, loyal to trusted people, and wary of easy judgment. A smaller number are openly idealistic and meet prejudice with public generosity rather than guardedness.
  • Size. Tieflings are about the same size and build as humans. Your size is Medium.
  • Speed. Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
  • Darkvision. Thanks to your infernal heritage, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
  • Hellish Resistance. You have resistance to fire damage.
  • Languages. You can speak, read, and write Common and Infernal.

Bloodline of Asmodeus

Tiefling bloodlines are family history, not a moral sentence. Different lineages have distinct customs, but each faces the City habit of treating infernal appearance as proof of danger.

  • Infernal Legacy. You know the Thaumaturgy cantrip. Once you reach 3rd level, you can cast the Hellish Rebuke spell once as a 2nd-level spell. Once you reach 5th level, you can also cast the Darkness spell once. You must finish a long rest to cast these spells again with this trait. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells.
