Paladin: Oath of the Ancients

The Oath of the Ancients is a vow to protect the old world of wonder, balance, and living beauty. Those who swear it often become guardians of sacred groves, forgotten ruins, and the quiet places where nature and myth still hold sway.

Channel Divinity

At 3rd level, you can use your Channel Divinity to surge with the strength of ancient lands.

Nature's Wrath. As a bonus action, you can expend your Channel Divinity to cause spectral vines, roots, and living energy to burst from the ground in a 10-foot radius centered on a point you can see within 60 feet. Creatures of your choice in the area must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be restrained until the end of your next turn.

Turn the Faithless. As a bonus action, you can expend your Channel Divinity to force aberrations, celestials, elementals, fey, fiends, and undead that can see or hear you within 30 feet to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the creature must use its reaction to move as far from you as it can and can't willingly move closer to you until the start of your next turn.

Aura of Warding

Starting at 7th level, you and friendly creatures within 10 feet of you have resistance to damage from spells. At 18th level, the range of this aura increases to 30 feet.

Undying Sentinel

At 15th level, when you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can drop to 1 hit point instead. This feature can't be used again until you finish a long rest.

When a creature you can see within 5 feet of you attacks someone other than you, you can use your reaction to make a weapon attack against the attacker. This reaction occurs only after an enemy attacks, not before.

Master of Nature

At 20th level, you can invoke the power of ancient magic to protect your allies and command the battlefield. As an action, you can transform into a creature of primal might, gaining the following benefits for 1 minute:

  • You gain resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.
  • You can cast Conjure Animals without expending a spell slot.
  • You can move through difficult terrain without penalty.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
