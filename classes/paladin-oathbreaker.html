CLASSES: Paladin: Oathbreaker

The Oathbreaker is the dark reflection of the paladin's sacred promise. Those who break their vow often seek to weaponize what they once held holy, turning discipline and divine authority into tools of fear and domination.

Channel Divinity

At 3rd level, you can channel corruption and dread into your weapon and your command.

Control Undead. As a bonus action, you can expend your Channel Divinity to force one undead creature you can see within 30 feet to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the creature becomes charmed by you until the end of its next turn.

Dreadful Aspect. As a bonus action, you can expend your Channel Divinity to project a terrifying aura. Each creature of your choice within 30 feet that can see you must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or become frightened of you until the end of your next turn.

Aura of Hate

Starting at 7th level, enemies within 10 feet of you have disadvantage on saving throws against fear effects, and when you deal damage with a weapon attack, the target takes psychic damage equal to your Charisma modifier. At 18th level, the aura's range increases to 30 feet.

Supernatural Resistance

At 15th level, you gain advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects that would impose the charmed, frightened, or stunned condition.

Dread Lord

At 20th level, as a bonus action, you can transform yourself into a terrifying icon of ruin for 1 minute. While this feature lasts, you gain the following benefits:

  • You gain resistance to psychic damage.
  • Enemies within 30 feet of you can't regain hit points.
  • When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can force it to make a Wisdom saving throw or become frightened of you until the end of your next turn.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
