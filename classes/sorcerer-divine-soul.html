Sorcerer: Divine Soul

A Divine Soul's magic comes from a divine source that glimmers within the soul. This lineage grants access to cleric spells and a favored affinity of divine power.

Divine Magic

Your link to the divine allows you to learn spells normally associated with the cleric class. When your Spellcasting feature lets you learn a sorcerer cantrip or spell of 1st level or higher, you can choose that spell from the cleric spell list or the sorcerer list. That spell becomes a sorcerer spell for you.

Favored by the Gods

At 1st level, if you fail a saving throw or miss with an attack, you can roll 2d4 and add it to the total, possibly changing the outcome. Once used, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

Empowered Healing

Starting at 6th level, when you or an ally within 5 feet regains hit points from a spell, you can spend 1 sorcery point to reroll any number of those dice once, provided you aren't incapacitated. Use once per turn.

Angelic Form

At 14th level, you can use a bonus action to manifest spectral wings. While present you have a flying speed of 30 feet. The wings last until you are incapacitated, die, or dismiss them.

Unearthly Recovery

At 18th level, when you have fewer than half your hit points remaining, as a bonus action you can regain a number of hit points equal to half your hit point maximum. Once used, you can't use it again until a long rest.
