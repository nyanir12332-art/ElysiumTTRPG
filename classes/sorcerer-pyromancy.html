Sorcerer: Pyromancy

A burning spark of elemental fire resides within you. Pyromancy sorcerers excel at dealing fire damage and shaping flames to their will.

Flameborn

At 1st level, you add your Charisma modifier to the damage of any cantrip that deals fire damage. In addition, you learn the produce flame cantrip if you don't already know it.

Inferno Adept

At 6th level, when you cast a spell that deals fire damage, you can spend 1 sorcery point to add additional fire damage equal to your Charisma modifier to a single target.

Blazing Mantle

At 14th level, you can surround yourself with burning mantle as a bonus action. For 1 minute, creatures that end their turn within 5 feet take fire damage equal to your Charisma modifier. You can end the effect as a bonus action.

Avatar of Flame

At 18th level, you can spend 6 sorcery points to gain immunity to fire damage for 1 minute and cause your spells that deal fire damage to deal maximum damage instead of rolling. Once used, you can't use it again until a long rest.
