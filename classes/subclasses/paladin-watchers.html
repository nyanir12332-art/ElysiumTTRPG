CLASSES: Paladin: Oath of the Watchers

The Oath of the Watchers is sworn by paladins who stand sentinel against the unearthly and the unseen. They watch for planar intrusions, extraplanar predators, and the subtle corruption that leaks through hidden seams in reality.

Channel Divinity

At 3rd level, your oath gives you the power to bind and discern what should not be allowed close to the world.

Abjure the Extraplanar. As a reaction when a creature within 30 feet of you that is from another plane of existence makes an attack roll, you can expend your Channel Divinity to turn that attack into a miss. The creature must then succeed on a Charisma saving throw or be stunned until the start of your next turn.

Watcher's Will. As a bonus action, you can expend your Channel Divinity to gain advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects until the start of your next turn.

Ward Against the Unseen

Starting at 7th level, creatures within 10 feet of you have advantage on saving throws against being charmed, frightened, or possessed. At 18th level, this aura increases to 30 feet.

Vigilant Rebuke

At 15th level, if a creature within 30 feet of you casts a spell, you can use your reaction to force it to make a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, the creature is unable to cast spells until the end of its next turn.

Mortal Bulwark

At 20th level, you become a living fortress against the unnatural. As a bonus action, you can assume a warded stance for 1 minute. While in this stance, you have resistance to all damage from spells, and when a creature within 30 feet of you is hit by a spell attack, you can use your reaction to impose disadvantage on the attack roll.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
