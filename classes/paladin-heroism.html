CLASSES: Paladin: Oath of Heroism

The Oath of Heroism is sworn by those who refuse to let fear decide what they do. These paladins are bold, inspirational, and often the first to step into danger to shield others from harm.

Channel Divinity

At 3rd level, you can use your Channel Divinity to inspire bold action and supernatural resolve.

Peerless Skill. As a bonus action, you can expend your Channel Divinity to gain advantage on one ability check made before the end of your turn. If the ability check is an attack roll or a saving throw, you instead gain a +5 bonus to that roll.

Inspirational Surge. As a bonus action, you can expend your Channel Divinity to grant one creature within 30 feet temporary hit points equal to your paladin level plus your Charisma modifier.

Aura of the Guardian

Starting at 7th level, when a creature within 10 feet of you takes damage, you can use your reaction to reduce the damage by your Charisma modifier. At 18th level, the range of this aura increases to 30 feet.

Radiant Presence

At 15th level, when you roll initiative and at the start of each of your turns, you can choose one creature within 30 feet of you that can hear you to gain advantage on its next attack roll or saving throw before the end of its next turn.

Champion of the Dawn

At 20th level, as a bonus action, you can gain the following benefits for 1 minute:

  • You gain a fly speed equal to your walking speed.
  • Whenever a creature within 30 feet of you hits with an attack, you can use your reaction to impose disadvantage on the attack roll.
  • When you hit with a weapon attack, you deal an extra 10 radiant damage.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
