Rogue: Swashbuckler

Swashbucklers excel in daring combat, combining panache and speed to dominate the duel and captivate onlookers.

Fancy Footwork

Starting at 3rd level, when you make a melee attack against a creature, that creature can't make opportunity attacks against you for the rest of your turn.

Rakish Audacity

Also at 3rd level, you gain a bonus to initiative equal to your Charisma modifier and you treat being alone with your target as granting Sneak Attack if no other creatures are within 5 feet of you and you don't have disadvantage.

Panache

At 9th level, you can use a bonus action to make a Charisma (Persuasion) check contested by a creature's Insight. On success you either charm the creature (if it fails) or goad it, and you gain advantage on attack rolls against that creature for 1 minute.

Elegant Maneuver

At 13th level, you can add your Charisma modifier to Dexterity checks made to escape grapples and to Acrobatics checks used to reposition in combat.

Master Duelist

At 17th level, when you make a successful Dexterity (Acrobatics) or Charisma (Performance) check during combat, you can spend your reaction to add your proficiency bonus to an attack roll or saving throw made before the end of your next turn.
