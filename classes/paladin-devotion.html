Paladin: Oath of Devotion

The Oath of Devotion binds its paladins to the ideals of discipline, honor, and selfless service. These champions stand as steadfast defenders of truth and compassion, leading by example even when the path before them is difficult.

Channel Divinity

At 3rd level, you can use your Channel Divinity to carry your oath into both offense and defense.

Sacred Weapon. As a bonus action, you can expend your Channel Divinity to imbue one weapon you hold with radiant energy. For 1 minute, the weapon glows with faint divine light and gains a bonus to attack and damage rolls equal to your Charisma modifier.

Turn the Unholy. As a bonus action, you can expend your Channel Divinity to force fiends and undead within 30 feet that can hear you to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, a creature is turned for 1 minute or until it takes damage.

Aura of Devotion

Starting at 7th level, you and friendly creatures within 10 feet of you gain advantage on saving throws against being charmed. At 18th level, the range of this aura increases to 30 feet.

Purity of Spirit

At 15th level, you gain immunity to being charmed, and you can use your reaction to end the frightened condition on yourself or a creature you can see within 30 feet.

Holy Nimbus

At 20th level, your presence becomes a sanctified aura. As a bonus action, you can gain the following benefits for 1 minute:

  • Bright light shines from you in a 30-foot radius and dim light 30 feet beyond that.
  • Enemies inside the bright light have disadvantage on attack rolls against you.
  • When you make a weapon attack, you can choose to deal radiant damage equal to your Charisma modifier to the target.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
