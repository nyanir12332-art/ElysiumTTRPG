Rogue: Scout

Scouts are rangers in rogue's clothing-skilled at moving ahead of their group, setting ambushes, and surviving in the wild.

Skirmisher

At 3rd level, you are adept at hit-and-run tactics. When you make a ranged attack, you can move up to half your speed without provoking opportunity attacks from the target of that attack.

Survivalist

You gain proficiency in Survival and Nature, and you can forage and navigate more effectively than most. You also gain expertise in one of those skills at 9th level.

Superior Mobility

Beginning at 9th level, your speed increases by 10 feet, and difficult terrain costs you no extra movement when you are traveling alone or acting as a scout ahead of an allied force.

Ambush Master

At 13th level, when you attack a creature that hasn't yet acted in combat, your attacks score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.

Sudden Strike

At 17th level, when you hit a surprised creature you may deal additional damage equal to your Sneak Attack dice and steal a momentary advantage, granting you temporary hit points equal to half that damage (rounded down).
