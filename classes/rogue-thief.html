Rogue: Thief

Thieves are nimble and resourceful, masters of stealth, locks, and quick hands.

Fast Hands

Starting at 3rd level, you can use the Bonus Action granted by your Cunning Action to make a Sleight of Hand check, use your thieves' tools to disarm a trap or open a lock, or take the Use an Object action.

Second-Story Work

At 3rd level, your climbing ability and athleticism improve. You gain a climbing speed equal to your walking speed when you move and take no fall damage from a jump that you make as part of your movement if you make a successful Acrobatics check.

Supreme Sneak

At 9th level, you have advantage on Stealth checks when you move no more than half your speed on the same turn.

Use Magic Device

At 13th level, you ignore class, race, and level requirements on the use of magic items that require them.

Thief's Reflexes

At 17th level, you can take two turns during the first round of any combat. You act on your initiative count twice, but you take your second turn immediately after your first.
