Sorcerer: Draconic Bloodline

Your innate magic comes from draconic blood that mingled with your ancestors' bloodline. The scale and breath of dragons mark your sorcerous gifts.

Dragon Ancestor

At 1st level, choose one type of dragon as your ancestor. The damage type associated with each dragon is used by features you gain later.

Draconic Resilience

Your hit point maximum increases by 1 and increases by 1 again whenever you gain a sorcerer level. Parts of your skin are covered by a thin sheen of dragonlike scales; when you aren't wearing armor, your AC equals 13 + your Dexterity modifier.

Elemental Affinity

Starting at 6th level, when you cast a spell that deals your ancestor's damage type, add your Charisma modifier to one damage roll of the spell. You can spend 1 sorcery point to gain resistance to that damage type for 1 hour.

Dragon Wings

At 14th level, you can sprout a pair of dragon wings as a bonus action, gaining a flying speed equal to your current speed. The wings last until you dismiss them as a bonus action.

Draconic Presence

At 18th level, you channel your dragon ancestry to exude awe or fear. As an action, spend 5 sorcery points to create an aura in a 60-foot radius; each hostile creature must succeed on a Wisdom save or be charmed or frightened for 1 minute. Creatures that succeed are immune to your aura for 24 hours.
