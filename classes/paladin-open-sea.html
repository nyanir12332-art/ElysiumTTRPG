CLASSES: Paladin: Oath of the Open Sea

The Oath of the Open Sea binds a paladin to the sky, wind, and endless horizon. These champions move as naturally on the deck of a ship or the crest of a wave as others do on solid ground.

Channel Divinity

At 3rd level, you can channel the energy of the sea to protect or command those around you.

Storm's Rebuke. As a reaction when a creature within 30 feet of you makes an attack roll, you can expend your Channel Divinity to force the creature to make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature is pushed 10 feet and loses the attack's bonus or damage.

Sea Legs. As a bonus action, you can expend your Channel Divinity to gain a fly speed of 30 feet and advantage on checks made to keep your footing on difficult terrain or while on a boat, for 1 minute.

Fathomless Step

Starting at 7th level, you gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed, and once per turn when you move, you can ignore difficult terrain caused by water, rubble, or broken decking. Creatures moving within 10 feet of you cannot easily keep track of your position, giving you advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made while at sea.

Stormwake

At 15th level, when you are subjected to a forced movement effect, you can use your reaction to move up to half your speed in a direction of your choice without provoking opportunity attacks.

You can also use your reaction to grant yourself a 10-foot bonus to movement when you take damage from a creature within 30 feet, representing a surge of momentum and grit.

Captain of the Horizon

At 20th level, you can spend 1 minute calling on the sea to grant you a commanding presence. Until the end of your next long rest, your allies gain advantage on saving throws made against fear effects while they are within 30 feet of you, and you can use your bonus action to command all creatures that can hear you within 30 feet to make a Wisdom saving throw or be charmed by your presence until the end of your next turn.
