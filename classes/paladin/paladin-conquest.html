CLASSES: Paladin: Oath of Conquest

The Oath of Conquest is a vow to dominate fear, weakness, and the chaos that spreads from tyrants and monsters alike. Its paladins do not merely resist evil-they break it with relentless discipline.

Channel Divinity

At 3rd level, you can assert relentless authority over the battlefield.

Conquering Presence. As a bonus action, you can expend your Channel Divinity to force each creature of your choice within 30 feet that can see or hear you to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, a creature becomes frightened of you until the end of its next turn.

Guided Strike. As a bonus action, you can expend your Channel Divinity to gain a bonus to a weapon attack roll equal to your proficiency bonus, before the roll is made.

Aura of Conquest

Starting at 7th level, enemies within 10 feet of you have disadvantage on saving throws against fear effects. At 18th level, the aura's range increases to 30 feet.

Scornful Rebuke

At 15th level, when a creature within 10 feet of you hits you with an attack, you can use your reaction to force it to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the creature is frightened of you until the end of its next turn.

Invincible Spirit

At 20th level, as a bonus action, you can gain the following benefits for 1 minute:

  • You gain resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.
  • You can add your Charisma modifier to weapon damage rolls.
  • When an enemy within 30 feet of you makes an attack, you can use your reaction to force it to have disadvantage on the attack roll.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
