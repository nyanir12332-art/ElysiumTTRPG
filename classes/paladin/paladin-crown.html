CLASSES: Paladin: Oath of the Crown

The Oath of the Crown binds the paladin to duty, law, and the steady hand of rightful authority. Those who take this oath are defenders of order, often standing between chaos and the common people they serve.

Channel Divinity

At 3rd level, you can use your Channel Divinity to impose discipline on the battlefield.

Champion Challenge. As a bonus action, you can expend your Channel Divinity to challenge a creature within 30 feet that can hear you. Until the end of your next turn, the creature has disadvantage on attack rolls against targets other than you, and it must make a Wisdom saving throw when it attacks you.

Turn the Tide. As a reaction when a creature within 30 feet of you takes damage, you can expend your Channel Divinity and grant that creature a bonus to its AC or a number of temporary hit points equal to your paladin level.

Aura of the Crown

Starting at 7th level, enemies within 10 feet of you have disadvantage on saving throws against effects that force them to move, and you have advantage on Charisma checks made to command or intimidate creatures that can hear you. At 18th level, the aura's range increases to 30 feet.

Unyielding Spirit

At 15th level, when a creature within 10 feet of you is forced to make a saving throw, you can use your reaction to impose advantage on the roll. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

Exalted Champion

At 20th level, your oath turns you into a symbol of lawful might. As a bonus action, you can gain the following benefits for 1 minute:

  • Your weapon attacks score a critical success on a roll of 19 or 20.
  • You gain resistance to psychic damage.
  • Once per turn, when you hit with a weapon attack, you can force the target to make a Wisdom saving throw or be frightened of you until the end of your next turn.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
