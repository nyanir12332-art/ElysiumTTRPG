CLASSES: Paladin: Oath of Glory

The Oath of Glory is a performance-driven contract that turns visible courage into public confidence. Its paladins work as champions, escorts, and morale figures whose successes make a patron, Office, or neighborhood look capable of surviving.

Channel Divinity

At 3rd level, you can use your Channel Divinity to turn your courage into a force that guides allies and overpowers enemies.

Peerless Athlete. As a bonus action, you can expend your Channel Divinity to gain a burst of speed and advantage on your next Athletics or Acrobatics check before the end of your turn.

Inspiring Smite. When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can expend your Channel Divinity to deal radiant damage and grant one creature you can see within 30 feet temporary hit points equal to your paladin level.

Aura of Alacrity

Starting at 7th level, allies within 10 feet of you can add your Charisma modifier to their initiative rolls, and your movement speed increases by 10 feet while you are not incapacitated. At 18th level, the range of this aura increases to 30 feet.

Shield of the Faithful

At 15th level, when you take the Dodge action, you can use your reaction to grant a creature within 30 feet advantage on the next attack roll it makes before the start of your next turn.

Living Legend

At 20th level, you become a mythic example of heroism. As a bonus action, you can choose to gain the following benefits for 1 minute:

  • You gain a fly speed equal to your walking speed.
  • You have resistance to all nonmagical damage.
  • Once on each of your turns, when you hit with a weapon attack, you can deal an extra 10 radiant damage.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
