CLASSES: Paladin: Oath of Vengeance

The Oath of Vengeance is a pursuit contract focused on a named harm, target, or unresolved violation. Its paladins are sent when an Office, Association, or victim cannot accept a case being forgotten, and their regimen keeps the oath directed rather than consuming them.

Channel Divinity

At 3rd level, you can channel your wrath with almost supernatural precision.

Abjure Enemy. As a bonus action, you can expend your Channel Divinity to force a creature you can see within 30 feet to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the creature is frightened of you until the end of its next turn.

Vow of Enmity. As a bonus action, you can expend your Channel Divinity to designate one creature you can see within 30 feet as your foe until the end of your next turn. You gain advantage on attack rolls against that creature.

Relentless Avenger

Starting at 7th level, when a creature within 30 feet of you makes an attack against a target other than you, you can use your reaction to move up to half your speed toward that creature. If you end this movement adjacent to the creature, you can make a melee attack against it.

Soul of Vengeance

At 15th level, when a creature you can see within 30 feet of you takes damage, you can use your reaction to deal a number of extra damage equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1) to that creature. This damage can only be dealt once per turn.

Avenging Angel

At 20th level, as a bonus action, you gain the following benefits for 1 minute:

  • You gain a flying speed equal to your walking speed.
  • When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you deal extra radiant damage equal to your Charisma modifier.
  • Once each round, when an enemy within 30 feet of you attacks, you can use your reaction to make one weapon attack against that creature.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
