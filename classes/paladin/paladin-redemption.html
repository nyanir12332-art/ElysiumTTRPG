CLASSES: Paladin: Oath of Redemption

The Oath of Redemption is a promise to end violence through compassion, restraint, and moral courage. These paladins try to break cycles of fear, hatred, and rage by standing between aggressor and victim.

Channel Divinity

At 3rd level, your oath is rooted in peace and restraint.

Emissary of Peace. As a bonus action, you can expend your Channel Divinity to soothe the hearts of creatures within 30 feet that can hear you. Each creature makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, a creature can't attack until the end of its next turn.

Rebuke the Violent. As a reaction when a creature within 30 feet of you attacks, you can expend your Channel Divinity to impose disadvantage on the attack roll. If the attack still hits, the damage is reduced by your paladin level.

Aura of the Guardian

Starting at 7th level, you and any creature you can see within 10 feet of you have advantage on Wisdom saving throws against fear. At 18th level, the range of this aura increases to 30 feet.

Protective Spirit

At 15th level, when you use your Channel Divinity to rebuke violence, you can also choose to grant yourself or an ally within 30 feet temporary hit points equal to your paladin level.

Final Rebuke

At 20th level, when you reduce a creature to 0 hit points, you can grant yourself and allies within 30 feet temporary hit points equal to your paladin level. You can use this feature once per long rest.
