CLASSES: Rogue: Assassin

Assassins are masters of infiltration, disguise, and death delivered quickly and precisely. They prepare thoroughly, strike without warning, and disappear into the shadows.

Bonus Proficiencies

When you choose this archetype at 3rd level, you gain proficiency with the disguise kit and the poisoner's kit. You also gain proficiency with the Stealth skill if you don't already have it.

Assassinate

Starting at 3rd level, you are at your deadliest when you get the drop on your foes. You have advantage on attack rolls against any creature that hasn't taken a turn in the combat yet. In addition, any hit you score against a creature that is surprised is a critical hit.

Infiltration Expertise

At 9th level, you can spend time creating a second identity. You can also mimic speech patterns and physical mannerisms of another person.

Impostor

At 13th level, you gain the ability to assume another's identity with uncanny skill. You gain advantage on Charisma (Deception) and Charisma (Performance) checks when trying to pass as that person.

Death Strike

Starting at 17th level, when you hit a creature that is surprised, you can treat the attack as if it were a critical hit. When you land a critical hit on a surprised creature, double the extra damage dice from Sneak Attack.
