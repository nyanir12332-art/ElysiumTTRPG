CLASSES: Sorcerer: Runechild

Runes hum beneath your skin; you channel those patterns to weave fluid magic and shape reality in striking, sigiled ways.

Rune Affinity

At 1st level, you learn two cantrips from the wizard spell list and inscribe a rune upon your skin. The rune grants a bonus to one skill of your choice equal to your proficiency bonus while it's active. The rune remains until you choose to replace it.

Runic Patterns
RuneEffect
Fire RuneAdd fire damage to spells equal to your Charisma modifier
Shield RuneGain +2 bonus to AC for 1 minute when activated
Swiftness RuneGain +10 ft movement speed for 1 minute
Stone RuneGain temporary HP equal to your sorcerer level

Runic Channel

At 6th level, you can use a bonus action to channel a rune, producing one of its effects without expending a spell slot. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and regain uses on a long rest.

Sigil Mastery

At 14th level, the runes you channel last longer and can be combined: channel two runes at once to gain both effects for 1 minute. Combining runes uses two uses of Runic Channel.

Ritual of the World-Scribe

At 18th level, once per long rest you can cast a 6th-level ritual-like effect that reshapes an area within 60 feet: you can change terrain, create a permanent minor architectural feature, or inscribe a powerful sigil that harms enemies who enter. The GM adjudicates specifics.
