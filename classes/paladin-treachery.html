Paladin: Oath of Treachery

The Oath of Treachery is taken by those who have turned away from honor and found power in betrayal. It is a path of deception, manipulation, and ruthless opportunism, prized by those who would rather survive than stand proud.

Channel Divinity

At 3rd level, your oath awakens cunning and cruelty in tandem.

Conjure Duplicate. As a bonus action, you can expend your Channel Divinity to create a shadowy duplicate of yourself within 30 feet. The duplicate lasts for 1 minute and acts on your initiative count. On your turn, you can command the duplicate to move or make a single weapon attack against a creature you can see at its current location.

Unholy Smite. When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can expend your Channel Divinity to deal an extra 3d8 psychic damage. If the target is frightened or charmed, it has disadvantage on the next saving throw it makes before the end of your next turn.

Aura of Treachery

Starting at 7th level, enemies within 10 feet of you have disadvantage on attack rolls against allies that are not you. At 18th level, the range of this aura increases to 30 feet.

Blackguard's Protection

At 15th level, when a creature within 30 feet of you is hit by an attack, you can use your reaction to force the attacker to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the creature is unable to attack anyone other than you until the end of its next turn.

Avatar of Treachery

At 20th level, as a bonus action, you can gain the following benefits for 1 minute:

  • You become invisible to creatures that cannot see you.
  • You can cast Mirror Image without expending a spell slot.
  • Once on each of your turns, when you hit with a weapon attack, you can force the target to make a Wisdom saving throw or be charmed by you until the end of your next turn.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
