  • Paladin

    Paladins gain power through binding contracts with orders, Offices under Associations, or organizations that answer directly to a Wing. Their oaths are maintained through monitored duties and specialized serums, drugs, or medications that stabilize the power while they work. An oathbreaker loses that support: the power purges their body through failed safeguards, and most wither and die. The few who live make the corrupted power their own, surviving by force of will rather than permission.

  • Warlock

    Warlocks obtain power through a negotiated source: a contract with a powerful being, a Prescript whose terms reshape the bearer, service through the Dodeka Association, or allegiance to a god or other entity from another plane. The terms may be written, spoken, ritual, or deliberately hidden, but a warlock must live with the relationship that keeps their power flowing.
