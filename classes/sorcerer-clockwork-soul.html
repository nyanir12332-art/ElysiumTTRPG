Sorcerer: Clockwork Soul

The cosmic force of order has suffused you with magic. That power arises from Mechanus or a realm like it - you are shaped by a vast and glorious system.

Clockwork Magic

You learn additional spells at certain levels, shown on the Clockwork Spells table. These spells are sorcerer spells for you and don't count against the number of sorcerer spells you know.

Manifestations of Order

Consult the Manifestations of Order table to choose or randomly determine a cosmetic manifestation of order while you are casting your sorcerer spells.

Restore Balance

Starting at 1st level, you can use your reaction to grant advantage or disadvantage to a creature that would otherwise roll with advantage or disadvantage when you can see it within 60 feet. Use a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus; regain uses on a long rest.

Bastion of Law

At 6th level, you can spend sorcery points to create a ward around a creature within 30 feet, represented by d8s equal to sorcery points spent; when the warded creature takes damage, you can roll those d8s and reduce the damage by their total. The ward lasts until you finish a long rest or use the feature again.

Trance of Order

At 14th level, as a bonus action you enter a trance for 1 minute. Attack rolls against you can't benefit from advantage and certain rolls you make can be treated as a 9 or lower on a d20 as a 10. Once used, can't use again until a long rest unless you spend 5 sorcery points.

Clockwork Cavalcade

At 18th level, you summon spirits of order in a 30-foot cube that provide a variety of effects (healing, repair, and damage mitigation for allies; restoring spells on 6th-level or lower creatures). Once used, you can't use again until a long rest unless you spend 7 sorcery points.
