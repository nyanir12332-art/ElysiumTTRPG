Rogue: Phantom

Phantoms are rogues haunted by the dead, channels for whispers and remnants of departed spirits. They draw on those voices and tokens to bolster their cunning and grant uncanny advantages.

Whispers of the Dead

Starting at 3rd level, you can channel whispers from the dead. When you finish a short or long rest, roll a d6 and record the result. You can expend the recorded whisper as a bonus action to add the roll to an ability check or saving throw.

Soul of the Departed

Also at 3rd level, you gain the ability to mark a token connected to a departed soul. When you hold a token—an object that once belonged to a dead creature—you gain advantage on one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw once before the token loses its power.

Tokens of the Departed

When you choose this archetype, you learn how to fashion soul tokens. A token is a small trinket you carry; a token's magic is expended when you use it to grant a benefit described by this archetype. Recover spent tokens after a long rest.

Wails from the Grave

At 9th level, you can call forth a mournful wail. As an action, choose a point you can see within 60 feet; each creature in a 10-foot radius must make a Wisdom saving throw or be frightened until the end of its next turn. Once you use this feature, you must finish a short or long rest to use it again.

Ghost Walk

At 13th level, you can briefly step partly into the spirit world. As a bonus action, you can become incorporeal until the end of your turn; while incorporeal you can move through creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain, but you end your movement in an unoccupied space. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1), regaining uses after a long rest.

Death's Friend

At 17th level, the barrier between you and the dead thins. When you die, you may return to life with hit points equal to your Sneak Attack dice + your Constitution modifier if a willing spirit intervenes (usable once per long rest). You gain other resistances to necrotic effects as your bond strengthens.
