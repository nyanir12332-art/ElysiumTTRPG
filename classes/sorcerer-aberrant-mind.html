Sorcerer: Aberrant Mind

An alien influence has wrapped its tendrils around your mind, giving you psionic power. You can touch other minds with that power and alter the world around you by using it to control the magical energy of the multiverse.

Aberrant Origins

As an Aberrant Mind sorcerer, you decide how you acquired your powers; roll on the table below for an origin idea.

Aberrant Origins
d6Origin
1You were exposed to the Far Realm’s warping influence; you believe a tentacle now grows on you, visible only to you.
2A psychic wind from the Astral Plane carried psionic energy to you; faint motes of light sparkle around you when you use your powers.
3You once suffered the dominating powers of an aboleth, leaving a psychic splinter in your mind.
4You were implanted with a mind flayer tadpole that never completed ceremorphosis; your flesh sometimes gleams with strange mucus when you use your powers.
5As a child, you had an imaginary friend that resembled a flumph or platypus-like creature; it gifted you with psionic powers.
6Your nightmares whisper the truth: your psionic powers are parasitic, drawn from a twin lodged in your mind.

Telepathic Speech

Starting at 1st level, you can form a telepathic connection with another creature you can see within 30 feet as a bonus action. You and the chosen creature can speak telepathically while you are within a number of miles equal to your Charisma modifier. Both creatures must understand a language mentally to converse.

Psionic Sorcery

Beginning at 6th level, when you cast a spell from your Psionic Spells feature, you can cast it by expending a spell slot as normal or by spending sorcery points equal to the spell’s level. These casts require no verbal or somatic components unless the spell consumes them.

Psychic Defenses

At 6th level, you gain resistance to psychic damage and advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened.

Revelation in Flesh

Beginning at 14th level, as a bonus action you can spend sorcery points to transform your body for 10 minutes. Each sorcery point grants one of the following until the transformation ends:

  • See invisible creatures within 60 ft (not through total cover).
  • Gain a flying speed equal to walking speed and hover; your skin may glisten.
  • Gain swimming speed equal to twice walking speed and breathe underwater; gills or webbed fingers may appear.
  • Your body becomes pliable and slim, allowing movement through spaces as narrow as 1 inch; spend 5 ft to escape nonmagical restraints.

Warping Implosion

At 18th level, you can teleport as an action to a space within 120 ft. Immediately after you disappear, each creature within 30 ft must make a Strength save vs your spell save DC or take 3d10 force damage and be pulled toward the space you left into an unoccupied space as close to your former space as possible. On success, the creature takes half and isn't pulled. Once used, you can't use it again until a long rest, unless you spend 5 sorcery points to use it again.
