CLASSES: Sorcerer: Storm Sorcery

Storm Sorcery is a sorcerous expression of power that emerges through bloodline, exposure, implantation, or a life-changing event. Its bearer must learn how to use that magic without letting an Office, Wing, or patron define their entire life.

Tempestuous Magic

Starting at 1st level, when you cast a spell of 1st level or higher you can use a bonus action to gain a flying movement of 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. The distance increases as you gain sorcerer levels.

Heart of the Storm

At 6th level, when you cast a spell that deals lightning or thunder damage, creatures of your choice within 10 feet take half the spell's damage as lightning or thunder damage.

Windborn

At 14th level, you gain resistance to lightning and thunder damage and your movement cannot be reduced by difficult terrain.

Maelstrom

At 18th level, you may, once per long rest, unleash a maelstrom in a 60-foot radius, dealing heavy lightning and thunder damage, knocking creatures prone, and potentially creating difficult terrain for 1 minute.
