CLASSES: Sorcerer: Giant Soul

Giant Soul is a sorcerous expression of power that emerges through bloodline, exposure, implantation, or a life-changing event. Its bearer must learn how to use that magic without letting an Office, Wing, or patron define their entire life.

Jotun Resilience

Your hit point maximum increases by 1 at 1st level and again whenever you gain a level in this class.

Mark of the Ordning

At 1st level, choose a giant type. At 1st and 3rd level you learn spells associated with that giant type; these spells don't count against your known sorcerer spells.

Soul of Lost Ostoria

At 6th level, you gain a benefit when casting your Mark of the Ordning spells (examples: Cloud Giant teleport, Fire Giant damage bonus, Frost Giant temporary hit points, Hill Giant targeting, Stone Giant AC bonus, Storm Giant lightning damage).

Rage of Fallen Ostoria

Starting at 14th level, when you cast a sorcerer spell and spend spell points equal to the cost of the spell, you can increase your size by one category for 1 minute and gain a suite of benefits: increased current hit points and maximum, +5 ft reach, +5 ft walking speed, advantage on Strength checks and saves, and bonus to melee damage equal to your Con modifier (min +1). Once used, you can't use again until a short or long rest.

Blessing of the All Father

At 18th level, your Constitution increases by 2, up to 22. You can use Rage of Fallen Ostoria twice between rests, but not more than once on a turn. The features stack if used together.
