CLASSES: Sorcerer: Lunar Sorcery

Lunar Sorcery is a sorcerous expression of power that emerges through bloodline, exposure, implantation, or a life-changing event. Its bearer must learn how to use that magic without letting an Office, Wing, or patron define their entire life.

Moonlit Novice

At 1st level, you learn to harness lunar energy. As a bonus action you can grant yourself a shimmering veil; until the start of your next turn, you have advantage on stealth checks and attackers have disadvantage on opportunity attacks against you.

Phases of Power

Beginning at 6th level, your lunar affinity affects your spells. When you cast an illusion or enchantment spell of 1st level or higher, you can spend 1 sorcery point to increase its duration by an amount equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1 round).

Moonward Spirit

At 14th level, call upon a lunar spirit to protect allies. As an action, you summon a spectral guardian in a 30-foot radius that grants allies advantage on Wisdom saving throws and reduces damage from necrotic and psychic sources by an amount equal to your Charisma modifier for 1 minute.

Ascendant Eclipse

At 18th level, once per long rest you can unleash an eclipse: choose a point you can see within 120 feet; creatures in a 30-foot radius must make a Constitution saving throw or be stunned for 1 round and take radiant damage equal to twice your sorcerer level. Successful saves take half damage and are not stunned.
