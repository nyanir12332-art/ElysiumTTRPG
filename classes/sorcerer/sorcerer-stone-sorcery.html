CLASSES: Sorcerer: Stone Sorcery

Stone Sorcery is a sorcerous expression of power that emerges through bloodline, exposure, implantation, or a life-changing event. Its bearer must learn how to use that magic without letting an Office, Wing, or patron define their entire life.

Stone's Endurance

Starting at 1st level, you can use a reaction to reduce damage dealt to you by 1d12 + your sorcerer level. Once used, you can't use this feature again until you finish a short or long rest.

Stoneform

At 6th level, you can use a bonus action to harden your skin, gaining resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing for 1 minute.

Quarrying Might

At 14th level, when you hit with a spell attack or force a creature to make a saving throw, you can cause the ground beneath them to erupt, dealing 4d6 bludgeoning damage and knocking them prone if they fail a Strength save.

Monolith's Stature

At 18th level, you gain immunity to non-Relic bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage. Additionally, once per long rest you can cast earthquake without spending spell points equal to the cost of the spell.
