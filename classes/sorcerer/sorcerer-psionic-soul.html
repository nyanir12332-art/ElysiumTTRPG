CLASSES: Sorcerer: Psionic Soul

Psionic Soul is a sorcerous expression of power that emerges through bloodline, exposure, implantation, or a life-changing event. Its bearer must learn how to use that magic without letting an Office, Wing, or patron define their entire life.

Telepathic Awareness

At 1st level, you can speak telepathically to any creature you can see within 30 feet. The creature needn't share a language with you, but it must be able to understand a language. This communication is limited to a few simple ideas.

Psychic Fortitude

At 6th level, you have advantage on saving throws against being charmed and frightened. In addition, you can spend 1 sorcery point to grant yourself temporary hit points equal to your sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier.

Manifest Mind

At 14th level, you can create a spectral avatar of your mind that assists in spellcasting. The avatar appears in an unoccupied space within 30 feet and lasts for 1 minute. While present it grants you advantage on concentration checks and once per turn you may add your Charisma modifier to a saving throw you force a creature to make.

Psionic Mastery

At 18th level, when you cast a spell that deals psychic damage, you can choose to reroll the damage dice and take the new result. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
