CLASSES: Rogue: Soulknife

Soulknife is a rogue specialization built around a particular kind of access, leverage, and timing. In the City it appears in information work, courier routes, investigations, illicit trade, and any job where a direct approach is unsafe.

Psionic Power

At 3rd level, you gain psionic energy expressed as psi points equal to your proficiency bonus. You can expend psi points to fuel psionic options detailed below.

Psychic Blades

Also at 3rd level, you craft a blade of psychic energy. This blade is a finesse weapon that uses Dexterity for attack and damage rolls. It counts as a simple melee weapon and deals 1d6 psychic damage plus your Dexterity modifier. Its damage increases as you gain levels.

Homing Strike

When you hit with your psychic blade you can spend a psi point to force the target to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure the target has disadvantage on its next attack roll before the end of its next turn.

Psychic Veil

At 9th level, you can spend psi points to turn invisible for a short time or shield your mind from divination, granting advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) or saving throws against being charmed.

Rend Mind

At 17th level, your psychic strikes can tear at the target's will. When you hit with your psychic blade you can spend 2 psi points to deal additional psychic damage equal to your rogue level and the target must make an Intelligence saving throw or be stunned until the end of your next turn.
