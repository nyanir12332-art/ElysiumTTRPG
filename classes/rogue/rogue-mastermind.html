CLASSES: Rogue: Mastermind

Masterminds specialize in social manipulation, intrigue, and battlefield tactics - using subtle control and deception to guide allies and confound enemies.

Master of Intrigue

At 3rd level, you gain proficiency with the disguise kit, forgery kit, and one gaming set of your choice. You also learn two languages of your choice.

Master of Tactics

Also at 3rd level, you can use the Help action as a bonus action, and you can do so at range equal to 30 feet if the target is an ally you can see and if the target's attack would benefit from Help.

Insightful Manipulator

At 9th level, you can spend an action to observe a creature for 1 minute; at the end, you gain advantage on social checks against that creature for 10 minutes.

Misdirection

At 13th level, when you succeed on a social skill check, you can transfer that success to an ally within 30 feet, granting them advantage on their next Charisma-based check.

Soul of Deceit

At 17th level, you gain immunity to being charmed and advantage on saving throws against being frightened. You also add double your proficiency bonus to Deception checks.
