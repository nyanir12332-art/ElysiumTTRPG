CLASSES: Rogue: Wild Card

Wild Cards are unpredictable tricksters, using chance and unusual tools to turn the tide in their favor.

Tricks Up the Sleeve

At 3rd level, you learn a variety of random gambits you can use in and out of combat. You carry a set of unusual items-loaded dice, a marked playing card, or a token from some obscure game-that you can expend to produce a small effect decided by the dealer.

Wild Card's Gambit

When you use a gambit you roll a d6 and consult the table below for the effect.

Wild Card Gambit Table
d6Effect
1Distract: grant advantage to one ally's attack roll against a creature within 30 ft.
2Slip Away: you gain +20 ft movement this turn and can Disengage as a free action.
3Loaded Strike: make a Sneak Attack with advantage; if it hits add 1d6 psychic damage.
4Wild Luck: reroll one ability check, attack roll, or saving throw and take the new result.
5Stun: target must succeed on a Con save or be stunned until the end of your next turn.
6Joker Wild: roll on this table again and double the effect where applicable.

Shifting the Odds

At 9th level, you can add a bonus equal to your proficiency bonus to a d20 roll you or an ally makes; you can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

Twist of Fate

At 13th level, when you roll a natural 1 on an attack roll, you can reroll it and must use the new result.

Joker Wild

At 17th level, your gambits become spectacular. When you roll a 6 on the Wild Card Gambit Table, you may choose the effect instead of doubling it, and you gain an extra use of Shifting the Odds per long rest.
