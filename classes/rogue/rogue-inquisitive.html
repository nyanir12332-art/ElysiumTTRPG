CLASSES: Rogue: Inquisitive

Inquisitives are investigators and interrogators, trained to pierce pretence and expose hidden truths.

Ear for Deceit

Starting when you choose this archetype at 3rd level, you gain proficiency in the Insight and Perception skills if you don't already have them. You also gain proficiency with one type of artisan's tools of your choice.

Eye for Detail

At 3rd level, you can use a bonus action to make a Perception or Investigation check related to hidden objects or traps. You may also use Insight checks to determine whether a creature is lying.

Insightful Fighting

At 3rd level, as a bonus action, you can make an Insight check contested by a creature's Deception check. If you succeed, you gain a bonus to attack rolls against that creature equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum +1) for 1 minute or until you use this feature again.

Steady Eye

At 9th level, you can add your Wisdom modifier to initiative rolls if you were surprised by an illusion or deception.

Unerring Eye

At 13th level, you can reroll a failed Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma check once per short rest.

Eye for Weakness

At 17th level, when you hit a creature you have Insightful Fighting on, the creature takes extra damage equal to your Sneak Attack dice once per turn.
