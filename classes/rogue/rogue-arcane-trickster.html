CLASSES: Rogue: Arcane Trickster

Some rogues enhance their fine-honed skills of stealth and agility with magic, learning tricks of enchantment and illusion. These rogues include pickpockets and burglars, but also pranksters, mischief-makers, and a significant number of adventurers.

Arcane Trickster Spellcasting

When you reach 3rd level, you augment your martial prowess with the ability to cast spells.

For this spellcasting table, divide your rogue level by three and round up. Use the result as your effective spellcaster level on the Spell Points by Level table.

Spellcasting

You learn three cantrips: Mage Hand and two other cantrips of your choice from the wizard spell list. You learn another wizard cantrip of your choice at 10th level.

Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for your wizard spells.

Mage Hand Legerdemain

Starting at 3rd level, when you cast Mage Hand, you can make the spectral hand invisible, and perform additional tasks with it, such as stow or retrieve objects worn by a creature, use thieves' tools at range, and more. You can also use your Cunning Action to control the hand.

Magical Ambush

Starting at 9th level, if you are hidden from a creature when you cast a spell on it, the creature has disadvantage on any saving throw it makes against the spell this turn.

Versatile Trickster

At 13th level, you can distract targets with Mage Hand. As a bonus action on your turn, you can designate a creature within 5 feet of the spectral hand; doing so gives you advantage on attack rolls against that creature until the end of your turn.

Spell Thief

At 17th level, you can magically steal a spell's knowledge from another spellcaster. After a creature casts a spell that targets you or includes you in its area, you can use your reaction to force the creature to make a saving throw with its spellcasting ability modifier. On a failed save, you negate the effect and steal the spell if it is of a level you can cast. For 8 hours you can cast the stolen spell using your spell points.
