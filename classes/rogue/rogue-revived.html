CLASSES: Rogue: Revived

You have been touched by death and returned changed. The memories of lives you never lived linger in your mind and your movements carry a whisper of the beyond.

Tokens of Past Lives

When you adopt this archetype at 3rd level, you gain a set of small tokens-trinkets that carry echoes of past existences. After a long rest you may attune to one token. While attuned to a token you gain proficiency with one skill or one set of tools of your choice. You can change which token is attuned when you finish a long rest.

Revived Nature

  • You have advantage on saving throws against disease and the poisoned condition.
  • You have resistance to poison damage.
  • You don't need to eat, drink, or breathe.
  • When you take a long rest, you need only 4 hours of inactivity instead of sleeping.

Bolts from the Grave

Also at 3rd level, you can make a special ranged attack as part of the Attack action or via Cunning Action. This attack uses Dexterity for the attack and damage rolls and is considered a magical attack. On a hit it deals necrotic damage equal to your Sneak Attack dice (once per turn). You are proficient with this attack.

Connect with the Dead

At 9th level, you can cast speak with dead without spending spell points equal to the cost of the spell or material components. When you do so, roll a d3 to determine an additional boon you gain until you finish a short or long rest:

Revived Capabilities
d3Benefit
1You learn one language of your choice.
2You gain proficiency with one skill or one tool of your choice.
3You gain proficiency with one saving throw of your choice.

Audience with Death

At 13th level, you gain advantage on saving throws against effects that would reduce you to 0 hit points. In addition, once per long rest while you are conscious, you may ask a single yes-or-no question of a spirit you can perceive; the spirit answers truthfully to the best of its knowledge.

Ethereal Jaunt

At 17th level, as a bonus action you can teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see, briefly stepping into the Ethereal Plane. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, regaining all uses on a long rest.
