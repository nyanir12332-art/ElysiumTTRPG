CLASSES: Sorcerer: Wild Magic

Your magic is unpredictable, and each casting can ripple with chaotic effects. Wild Magic sorcerers embrace this volatility to surprising ends.

Wild Surge

Immediately after you cast a sorcerer spell of 1st level or higher, the DM can ask you to roll on the Wild Magic Surge table. The table contains a range of effects, from harmless to potent; roll a d100 to determine the effect. You gain a number of additional wild surge uses equal to your Charisma modifier on a long rest.

Wild Surge Examples
RollEffect
01-05You heal 2d10 hit points.
06-15You cast feather fall on yourself.
16-30A random creature in 30 feet grows a random animal feature for 1 minute.
31-60You turn invisible for 1 minute or until you attack or cast a spell.
61-95Nothing happens.
96-100You cast a 6th-level spell centered on yourself but the target is random.

Tides of Chaos

Starting at 1st level, you can gain advantage on one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw. Once you use this feature, you must finish a long rest before using it again. The DM can have you regain the use of this feature when they see fit, especially when your wild magic surges.

Bend Luck

At 6th level, you can spend 2 sorcery points to roll 1d4 and add or subtract the number rolled from another creature's attack roll, ability check, or saving throw within 30 feet. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

Controlled Chaos

At 14th level, you can spend 3 sorcery points to choose an effect from the Wild Surge table and apply it instead of rolling, once per long rest.

Atlas of Possibilities

At 18th level, you can manipulate probability broadly: once per long rest you can force a reroll of any die rolled within 60 feet and choose the result you prefer. Creatures with advantage or disadvantage are unaffected by this ability.
